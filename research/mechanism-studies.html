Research / Mechanism Studies

The mechanism behind the headline finding

The flagship report showed that store readiness does not predict recommendation. These seven studies dig into why, each one testing a single variable such as web search, brand fame, domain recognition, self justification, vocabulary, intent, or the model's own memory.

7 published studies

Isolating one variable at a time

Each study holds the model and the prompts constant and changes exactly one thing, so the effect can be attributed with confidence rather than guessed at.
