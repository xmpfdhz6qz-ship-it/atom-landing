The Other Zero
0
Recorded AI Visits

We expected at least one. Then we found a bug in our own instrumentation.

No AI bot has ever visited

We checked our own AI bot visit log, the same instrumentation this series has used to study crawler behavior elsewhere. Across every domain it monitors, founder.atomfoundry.dev does not appear once. Not GPTBot, not ClaudeBot, not PerplexityBot, not any of the agents we track. Not one recorded visit, ever.

We are not reading too much into this. The dedicated storefront tracker that should log every hit on this specific store currently shows zero rows across every store it watches, not just ours, which tells us as much about the state of our own instrumentation as it does about AI traffic. We are treating that as a separate, honest bug to fix, not folding it into this result. Even accounting for that, a brand new store with no training data footprint, no recommendation history, and almost no outside web presence is exactly the profile our own research predicts would see nothing yet.

What We Learned

Three lessons from day zero.

Lesson 001

Technical accessibility does not guarantee AI discovery.

Lesson 002

Recommendation history does not exist yet.

Lesson 003

Intent is currently the only noisy factor.
