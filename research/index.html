Key Findings
52% of 500 analyzed stores score below 50/100 — classified as AI Invisible Risk. When buyers ask AI shopping agents for recommendations, these stores are systematically excluded from the answer.
2026 AI Readiness Report · 500 stores · May 2026

2026 AI Readiness Report · 500 stores · May 2026

Our Methodology

Atom Foundry evaluates ecommerce stores across 8 factors that determine how confidently AI shopping agents can understand, trust, and recommend a store. Our analysis is systematic, data-driven, and updated continuously as we scan new stores.
