What is an AI Commerce Audit

More than a score — a revenue map

AI Commerce Audit
An AI Commerce Audit is a comprehensive analysis of how AI shopping agents see, evaluate, and decide whether to recommend your Shopify store. It identifies every missing signal, quantifies the revenue impact of each gap, and provides prioritized fixes.

Unlike a generic SEO audit, an AI Commerce Audit focuses exclusively on the signals that AI shopping agents evaluate — schema markup, trust confidence, semantic clarity, commerce accuracy, and agentic infrastructure. These are the signals that determine whether ChatGPT, Alexa for Shopping, and Google AI Mode recommend your store or skip it entirely.

What the audit covers

Every signal that determines your AI recommendation probability

🏗️
AI Structured Signals
Schema markup completeness: Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, AggregateRating, price and currency. The single biggest factor. Average store scores 9/20.
🎯
Semantic Clarity
Can AI understand what you sell, to whom, and why you are different? Hero messaging, USP clarity, niche positioning, and content specificity.
🛡️
AI Trust Confidence
Machine-readable trust: AggregateRating schema, return policy text, shipping info in HTML, SSL, contact information. 72% of stores fail this check.
💰
Commerce Accuracy
Price visible without JavaScript, stock availability readable, variant structure clear, no hidden fees. Invisible prices = excluded from AI price comparisons.
⚡
Agentic Infrastructure
llms.txt quality, llms-full.txt completeness, agents.md configuration. Shopify generates these automatically but content quality determines your score.
🔍
AI Interpretability
Technical crawlability, image alt coverage, internal link structure, mobile viewport, no JS-only content blocks.

AI Commerce Audit questions

How long does the AI Commerce Audit take?▾
The full AI Commerce Audit completes in 30 seconds. Enter your domain URL, and the scan analyzes your store across all 8 factors in real time. No waiting, no email required, results displayed immediately.
Is the AI Commerce Audit really free?▾
Yes. The full AI Readiness Score audit is free, requires no signup, and no credit card. You get your complete score across all 8 factors, the factor breakdown, and your top priority fixes at no cost.
How is an AI Commerce Audit different from an SEO audit?▾
An SEO audit analyzes keyword rankings, backlinks, page speed, and content for search engine optimization. An AI Commerce Audit analyzes the signals AI shopping agents use to evaluate and recommend stores — schema markup quality, trust confidence, semantic clarity, commerce accuracy, and agentic infrastructure. These are fundamentally different signal sets. A store can pass an SEO audit and fail an AI Commerce Audit completely.
How often should I run an AI Commerce Audit?▾
Run an audit any time you make significant changes to your store — new theme, new apps, updated product pages, or policy changes. Also run one after implementing fixes from a previous audit to verify improvements. AI agents re-crawl stores frequently, so improvements show up in your score within days.
