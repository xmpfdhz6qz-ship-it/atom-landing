How AI recommends in activewear, and where one premium brand actually stands

An example report: what ChatGPT recommends when a shopper asks for activewear, where this brand lands, who wins instead, and the language gap that explains why.
How this was measured: We asked ChatGPT 20 high-intent activewear shopping questions, each run 20 times to capture variance, for 400 recommendation observations. For every answer we recorded which brands were recommended and in what position, and the reason the model gave. The client brand is anonymized; competitor names are shown as the model recommended them. Measured on ChatGPT; other AI models are being added.
Executive summary · for the CEO

When shoppers ask AI what activewear to buy, this brand is named in 15% of conversations. It is completely absent from the other 85%.

The brands AI recommends ahead of it, Nike, Under Armour, and Adidas, do not win on product quality. They win because AI understands them as performance brands. AI understands this brand as a fashion brand, so it quietly routes every performance question elsewhere.

This is not a visibility problem that more ads or SEO will fix. It is a recommendation problem: AI has already decided what this brand is for, and it is leaving most of the category on the table. Based on 400 measured recommendations in this category alone.

Part 2 · The why · the language gap that explains the position
Competitive language gap · most actionable

AI describes the winners and this brand in completely different words

These are the actual reasons ChatGPT gave when recommending each brand. This is the model's own language, not our interpretation. The gap between the two columns is the whole story.
What AI says about the winners
moisture-wicking technology cushioning & support durability stability for heavy lifts performance fabrics built for intense workouts
What AI says about this brand
stylish fashionable chic comfortable for casual wear perfect for yoga fashion-forward
AI has filed this brand under fashion, not performance. That one framing decides everything that follows. The brand wins the lifestyle questions like yoga and leggings, and disappears from every performance question like gym wear, moisture wicking, and training, because AI does not connect it to that language at all.

What this means for a brand

The old question was "can people find us?" The new question is "does AI recommend us, and as what?" Those are different problems with different answers.

This brand does not have a quality problem or a visibility problem. It has a framing problem. AI has decided what it is for, and that decision, not the product, governs which half of the category it gets to compete in.

What is measurable today: exactly where a brand stands, who beats it, in which questions it is absent, and the language AI ties to the winners. We do not claim a button to move a brand up on command. We make the invisible decision visible, which is the first thing anyone has to see before they can act on it.

Scope and honesty. This is an example report built from real measured data in the activewear category. It reflects one model (ChatGPT) at one point in time.
