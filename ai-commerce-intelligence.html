What is AI Commerce Intelligence?

AI Commerce Intelligence™ · Core Definition
AI Commerce Intelligence is the practice of measuring, understanding, and improving how AI shopping systems, including ChatGPT, Alexa for Shopping, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity, discover, evaluate, and recommend ecommerce stores to buyers. It is the intelligence layer between ecommerce infrastructure and the AI recommendation economy.

The internet is undergoing a structural shift. For twenty years, discovery ran through Google. A buyer searched, Google returned ten links, and the buyer chose. That model is ending. AI shopping agents now answer buying questions directly, and they choose which stores to recommend.

AI Commerce Intelligence is the discipline that measures this new layer. Not SEO, not GEO, not AI optimization. A complete intelligence infrastructure, dataset, scoring, monitoring, and recommendations, for the AI recommendation economy.

The Framework

AI selects stores based on three layers

AI shopping agents don't recommend randomly. They evaluate every store across three dimensions before making a recommendation. Understanding all three is what AI Commerce Intelligence makes possible.

🏛️
Authority
AI Authority Score™
What does the internet say about this brand? Backlinks, Reddit mentions, LinkedIn citations, press, reviews, third-party validation.
🎯
Relevance
AI Relevance Score™
Does this store answer the exact buyer query? Intent match, semantic overlap, topical depth, buyer-fit, category precision.
⚙️
Extractability
AI Extractability Score™
Can AI easily pull the answer? H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, FAQ schema, clean structure, server-side rendering, JSON-LD markup.

Getting all three aligned is where most stores fail, and where the gap is. Most stores optimize for humans and Google. Very few optimize for AI retrieval systems. That is the AI Commerce Intelligence opportunity.

FAQ

AI Commerce Intelligence: common questions

What is AI Commerce Intelligence?▾
AI Commerce Intelligence is the practice of measuring, understanding, and improving how AI shopping systems, including ChatGPT, Alexa for Shopping, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity, discover, evaluate, and recommend ecommerce stores to buyers. It is the intelligence layer between ecommerce infrastructure and the AI recommendation economy.
How is AI Commerce Intelligence different from SEO?▾
SEO optimizes for keyword rankings in Google search results. AI Commerce Intelligence optimizes for recommendation probability across AI shopping agents. AI agents evaluate structured data, trust signals, semantic clarity, and intent match, not keyword density or backlinks. A store can rank number one on Google and still be completely invisible to AI shopping agents.
What is the AI Commerce Score™?▾
The AI Commerce Score™ is a 0-100 composite score measuring how well an ecommerce store can be understood, trusted, and recommended by AI shopping agents. It measures 8 factors: Semantic Clarity, AI Readability, AI Interpretability, Recommendation Confidence, AI Trust Confidence, Commerce Accuracy, AI Crawlability, and External Authority Signals. Scores above 85 are Highly Recommendable. Below 50 is AI Invisible Risk.
What is Recommendation Share™?▾
Recommendation Share™ is the percentage of high-intent buying prompts in which a brand appears in AI recommendations. For example, your brand appears in 18% of high-intent buying prompts in the Beauty niche. It is the AI-era equivalent of search market share and one of the most important metrics AI Commerce Intelligence tracks. Learn more about Recommendation Share™.
Who needs AI Commerce Intelligence?▾
Any ecommerce brand that wants to be recommended by AI shopping agents needs AI Commerce Intelligence. As AI-referred orders grow 13x year-over-year per Shopify Q1 2026 data, stores invisible to AI systems are losing a growing share of revenue. Any brand in Fashion, Beauty, Health, Food, Home, Sports, Pets, Tech, Kids, or Jewelry is affected. The average AI Commerce Score across 6,789 scanned stores is 45/100, meaning most stores are already losing AI-driven revenue.
What is Prompt Visibility Testing™?▾
Prompt Visibility Testing™ is the process of sending real buyer prompts to AI systems, "best protein powder for women," "affordable luxury skincare," "eco-friendly dog food", and measuring whether a brand appears, how often, at what position, and who appears instead. It is the most direct measurement of AI Commerce Intelligence, showing exactly where a brand is visible and invisible in AI recommendation flows. Learn more about Prompt Visibility Testing™.
