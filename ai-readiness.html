What is the AI Readiness Score

A score that tells you exactly where you stand

AI Readiness Score
A 0-100 composite score measuring how confidently AI shopping agents can discover, understand, trust, and recommend your Shopify store to buyers. Higher score = higher probability of AI recommendation.

The AI Readiness Score is not a vanity metric. It directly predicts whether AI shopping agents like Alexa for Shopping, ChatGPT, and Google AI Mode will include your store in their recommendations when buyers ask relevant questions. Stores below 50 are systematically excluded. Stores above 85 are Highly Recommendable.

The 8 scoring factors

What the score actually measures

20
AI Structured Signals
Schema markup quality: Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, AggregateRating, price and currency schema. This is the single biggest factor. Average score: 9/20.
18
Semantic Clarity
Can AI understand what you sell, to whom, and why you are different? Hero specificity, USP clarity, description quality, FAQ presence.
18
AI Trust Confidence
Machine-readable trust signals: reviews with AggregateRating schema, return policy in crawlable text, SSL, shipping information, contact details.
14
User Intent Match
Clear niche positioning for specific buyer queries. Price tier communicated. Target customer explicit. Luxury, eco, minimalist, premium signals.
12
AI Interpretability
Technical crawlability: robots.txt, sitemap, mobile viewport, no JS-only content blocks, image alt coverage, internal link structure.
10
Commerce Accuracy
Price visible in HTML without JavaScript, schema:price and priceCurrency, stock availability visible, variant structure clear.
5
Recommendation Confidence
Delivery info visible, return process clear, no subscription traps, what-you-see-is-what-you-get purchase experience.
3
External Authority Signals
About page, social links, founder presence, consistent brand entity. AI cross-references external mentions to verify trust.

FAQ

AI Readiness Score questions

How is the AI Readiness Score calculated?▾
The AI Readiness Score is calculated by scanning your store across 8 factors: AI Structured Signals (20pts), Semantic Clarity (18pts), AI Trust Confidence (18pts), User Intent Match (14pts), AI Interpretability (12pts), Commerce Accuracy (10pts), Recommendation Confidence (5pts), and External Authority Signals (3pts). The total is 100 points maximum. See the full methodology for details.
What is a good AI Readiness Score?▾
The average across 4,300+ scanned stores is 45/100. A score above 70 is Moderately Recommendable. Above 85 is Highly Recommendable. Currently, not one store in our dataset has reached 85 or above. Most stores have significant room to improve.
How do I improve my AI Readiness Score?▾
The fastest improvements: add Product JSON-LD schema markup, enable AggregateRating schema for reviews, make prices visible in HTML, add return policy in crawlable text, and improve niche positioning. Run a free scan at atomfoundry.dev to see your exact gaps and dollar impact per issue.
How often is the AI Readiness Score updated?▾
Atom Foundry scans stores continuously. Your score reflects your store at the time of the most recent scan. As you make improvements, you can re-scan your store to see your updated score. Scores are not cached for more than 30 days.
