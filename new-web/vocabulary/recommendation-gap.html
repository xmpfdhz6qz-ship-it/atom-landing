What Is It

Not a score. Missing market share.

The Recommendation Gap is the quantified difference between a brand's current AI Recommendation Share and the share held by the top competitor in its category. It represents the revenue opportunity available through AI Commerce optimization. A large Recommendation Gap means significant revenue is flowing to competitors through AI recommendation channels.

Why AI Cares

The Recommendation Gap translates AI visibility data into commercial stakes. A brand with 12 percent Recommendation Share competing against a category leader at 67 percent has a 55-point gap. Every point of gap is estimated revenue flowing to a competitor through AI channels.

Example

A protein supplement brand appears in 14 percent of relevant buyer prompts. The category leader appears in 61 percent. The Recommendation Gap is 47 points. Closing it is the commercial case for AI Commerce optimization investment.

FAQ

Questions about Recommendation Gap

What is the Recommendation Gap?
The Recommendation Gap is the quantified difference between a brand's current AI Recommendation Share and the share held by the top competitor in its category. A brand at 14 percent competing against a leader at 61 percent has a 47-point gap.
Is the Recommendation Gap the same as Recommendation Share?
No. Recommendation Share is your own number, the percentage of buyer prompts where you appear. The Recommendation Gap is a comparison, the distance between your number and the category leader's number. Share tells you where you stand. Gap tells you what is actually at stake.
Why does the Recommendation Gap matter more than a percentage?
A percentage is abstract. A gap is a market. Every point of gap represents buyer prompts where AI is recommending a competitor instead of you, which is estimated revenue moving through AI channels to someone else.
Can the Recommendation Gap shrink without your share changing?
Yes. The gap is relative. If a competitor's share drops while yours holds steady, the gap narrows even though nothing changed on your end. This is why gap should always be tracked alongside your own Recommendation Share, not instead of it.
How is the Recommendation Gap closed?
By closing the causes behind it, not the number itself: stronger trust signals, complete schema markup, tighter intent match between buyer language and product language, clearer positioning, and higher recommendation confidence. The gap moves as an effect of those, not a direct lever.
