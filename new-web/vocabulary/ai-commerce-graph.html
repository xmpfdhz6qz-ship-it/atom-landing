    What Is It

    The map AI reads before it recommends.

    The AI Commerce Graph is the network of relationships AI systems build between businesses, products, trust signals, mentions, and buyer intent. It is the structure ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and Alexa for Shopping search before recommending anything, not a list of ranked pages.

    Every recommendation is really a route through this graph. Intent connects to a category, a category connects to products, and a product connects to a business with the right trust signals attached. A brand's position inside that network, not its keyword ranking, decides how often it gets recommended.
