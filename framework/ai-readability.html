Layer 01 · The Foundation

AI Readability™

Can AI read your business?

AI systems cannot understand what they cannot read. AI Readability is the first and most foundational layer of the AI Commerce Intelligence Framework™. It measures how accessible and machine-readable your business is to the crawlers and models behind ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Apple Intelligence. Without readability, none of the layers above it are possible: there is no understanding, no trust, and no recommendation.

What it measures

The five signals of AI Readability

Readability is not one thing. It is the combination of whether your data exists in a machine-readable form, whether the important parts are in the HTML rather than hidden, and whether AI crawlers are allowed to reach them in the first place. Each signal has its own page.

Structured DataView signal →
Structured Data measures how effectively products, pages, reviews, organizations, and business entities are described using machine-readable markup. AI systems rely on structured signals to identify entities, attributes, relationships, and business context.
Product DataView signal →
Product Data measures the completeness and quality of product information available to AI systems. Missing attributes, weak descriptions, and incomplete specifications reduce machine understanding.
Content StructureView signal →
Content Structure measures how clearly information is organized within the HTML document. Well structured content improves extraction, chunking, and retrieval.
CrawlabilityView signal →
Crawlability measures whether AI crawlers can discover, access, and retrieve content without restrictions.
AccessibilityView signal →
Accessibility measures whether content is available regardless of device, browser, or assistive technology. Accessible websites are often easier for machines to process.
Why it matters

Readability is the floor, not the ceiling

Every layer above this one depends on it. A business can have the best products, the strongest reviews, and the clearest positioning, but if an AI crawler cannot read the page, none of that exists from the machine's point of view. The store is simply skipped.

The principle

A weak readability layer caps everything above it. There is no understanding without readability, no trust without understanding, and no recommendation without trust. This is why AI Readability sits at the base of the framework.

Example: A store with a clean H1, server-rendered product descriptions, and Product schema is highly readable. The same store, where prices and descriptions load only after JavaScript runs, is nearly invisible to AI on the first crawl pass, no matter how good the products are.
