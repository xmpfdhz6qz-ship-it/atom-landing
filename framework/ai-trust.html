Layer 03 · Credibility

AI Trust™

Can AI trust your business?

Trust has always mattered. The difference now is that machines evaluate it alongside humans. AI Trust is Layer 03 of the AI Commerce Intelligence Framework™. It measures the signals that make a business look credible to an AI system: reviews, mentions, authority, reputation, and consistency. Trust directly influences whether you are recommended at all.

Layer 03 in the AI Commerce Intelligence Framework™ · click to enlarge
What it measures

The five signals of AI Trust

Trust is built from signals AI can actually verify, not from visual badges a human sees. It is the combination of what you show and what the rest of the web says about you. Each signal has its own page.

ReviewsView signal →
Reviews measures the quality, quantity, recency, and consistency of customer reviews available to AI in a machine-readable form.
Brand MentionsView signal →
Brand Mentions measures third party references, citations, discussions, and mentions of your business across the web.
Authority SignalsView signal →
Authority Signals measures expertise, recognition, backlinks, citations, and industry presence that mark your business as credible.
ReputationView signal →
Reputation measures sentiment, customer satisfaction, complaints, and public perception as AI can read them.
ConsistencyView signal →
Consistency measures whether business information, claims, branding, and customer experience stay aligned everywhere AI encounters you.
Why it matters

Trust is a gate before recommendation

AI does not recommend what it cannot vouch for. Before a business is considered for recommendation, its trust signals are weighed. Weak or missing trust pulls a business out of the running even when everything below it is strong.

The principle

Trust builds on understanding and feeds recommendation. A business AI does not trust will not be recommended, regardless of how readable or well understood it is.

Example: Two brands sell the same product. One has parseable reviews, press mentions, and a consistent identity across the web. The other has a polished site but no external footprint. AI trusts and recommends the first.
