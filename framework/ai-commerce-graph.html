The Core Entities

What the graph is built from

The AI Commerce Graph™ is built from six entity types. Together, they form the structure AI systems search when deciding who and what to recommend.

Businesses

Every business becomes a unique entity inside the graph.

The graph stores how AI identifies, understands, and connects businesses across different contexts.

Examples
  • Brand identity
  • Website
  • Industry
  • Authority
  • Reputation

Products

Products are the objects AI recommends.

Each product carries semantic attributes that help AI understand what it is, who it is for, and when it should be recommended.

Examples
  • Product name
  • Features
  • Price
  • Availability
  • Attributes

Categories

Categories organize commerce into meaningful groups.

They allow AI systems to connect similar products and businesses to relevant customer intent.

Examples
  • Coffee
  • Supplements
  • Beauty
  • Home
  • Fashion

Mentions

Mentions represent where and how businesses appear across AI-accessible content.

Not every mention is equal. The graph measures frequency, context, source quality, and semantic relevance.

Examples
  • Articles
  • Reviews
  • Forums
  • Research
  • Social content

Trust Signals

Trust signals help AI estimate whether a business is reliable.

These signals influence confidence, not just visibility.

Examples
  • Structured Data
  • Business Information
  • Policies
  • Reviews
  • Brand Consistency
  • Technical Quality

Recommendations

Recommendations are the observable output of the graph.

Every recommendation represents the combined effect of hundreds of connected signals.

We measure
  • Recommendation Frequency
  • Recommendation Position
  • Recommendation Share™
  • Recommendation Consistency
