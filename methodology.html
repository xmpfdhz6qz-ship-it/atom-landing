Scoring Model v2.0

How the AI Recommendation Score is calculated

The AI Recommendation Score is a 0–100 composite score across 8 factors. Each factor is weighted by its proven impact on AI agent recommendation behavior — informed by how Alexa for Shopping, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT Shopping, Perplexity, and Shopify AI evaluate stores.

A score below 50 means your store is effectively invisible in AI-driven discovery. A score above 85 means AI agents can confidently understand, trust, and recommend you. There is no middle ground.

Factor Definitions

The 8 factors: what we measure and why

01
AI Structured Signals
20% weight
Missing structured signals is the single biggest revenue leak in AI discovery. 72% of stores we scanned have this gap.

JSON-LD structured data is the primary language AI agents use to understand what a store sells, at what price, and how trustworthy it is. Shopify confirmed it: your store is becoming a product database for AI agents. If that database isn't structured, Alexa for Shopping, ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity can't read it — and they skip the store.

This factor measures the presence and completeness of schema markup including Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, AggregateRating, and llms.txt readiness. Each missing signal directly reduces AI recommendation confidence.

Product schemaOrganization schemaAggregateRatingllms.txtBreadcrumbListJSON-LD formatInventory signalsVariant structure
02
Semantic Clarity
18% weight

AI doesn't evaluate quality the way a human does. AI needs to understand: what does this store sell, to whom, why is it different, and what problem does it solve. Generic messaging scores zero. Specific positioning like "minimalist skincare for sensitive skin" scores high.

This factor measures hero clarity, category clarity, USP clarity, product description specificity, benefit-focused copy, FAQ presence, and semantic consistency across pages.

Specific hero messagingClear USPBenefit-focused copyDescription 150+ wordsFAQ presenceImage alt textsSemantic consistency
03
AI Trust Confidence
18% weight
64% of stores we scanned have missing or unreadable trust signals. Agents skip stores they cannot verify as trustworthy.

AI won't risk recommending a store it can't verify as trustworthy. Trust signals must be crawlable and structured, not just visually present on the page.

This factor measures review count and schema, return policy text, shipping clarity, SSL, trust badges, contact information visibility, and overall transparency.

Reviews + AggregateRatingReturn policy visibleShipping claritySSL certificateTrust badgesContact infoCompany identity
04
User Intent Match
New in v2.0
14% weight

AI doesn't rank stores generally — it ranks them per specific buyer intent. When a buyer says "find me a luxury minimalist skincare brand for sensitive skin under $80," AI needs to match that exact intent to a store.

Generic stores without clear niche positioning fail this test completely. Stores with explicit intent signals — luxury, eco, minimalist, premium, handmade, fast shipping — are significantly more likely to be selected for specific buyer queries.

Niche positioning clarityPrice tier signalingUse-case specificityTarget audience explicitValue propositionDifferentiators visible
05
AI Interpretability
12% weight

AI doesn't see your store the way a human does. It reads structure, hierarchy, and meaning. A technically inaccessible store — slow load times, JS-rendered content, popup overload — cannot be fully indexed by AI agents, regardless of content quality.

This factor measures Core Web Vitals, presence of sitemap.xml and robots.txt, mobile viewport, page hierarchy, and navigation structure.

robots.txt presentsitemap.xml presentPageSpeed 70+Mobile viewportLoad time 3s or lessNo JS-hidden content
06
Commerce Accuracy
10% weight
58% of stores are invisible to AI price comparisons due to inaccurate or hidden commerce data.

AI agents won't recommend a product that's out of stock, at the wrong price, or with confusing variants. Commerce accuracy means your product data is reliable, current, and machine-readable at all times.

Price in HTML (no JS)schema:price + priceCurrencyRealtime stock accuracyCurrency label visibleVariant structure clear
07
Recommendation Confidence
New in v2.0
5% weight

Measures how safely AI can predict that a customer will be satisfied after buying from this store. AI agents are being trained to optimize for user satisfaction, not just clicks. A store that generates complaints or disappointment will be deprioritized over time.

Delivery confidence signalsPurchase friction lowReturn process clearPayment options visiblePromise vs. reality match
08
External Authority Signals
New in v2.0
3% weight

AI won't only trust what a store says about itself. It will increasingly cross-reference the wider internet: Reddit discussions, YouTube reviews, TikTok content, blog mentions, creator endorsements. This is the AI Authority Graph — a reputational map of the internet that AI agents are building right now.

Currently scored at 3% weight as this signal is early-stage. However, we predict this will become one of the most important factors by 2027. Stores with strong external mentions will have a compounding authority advantage.
