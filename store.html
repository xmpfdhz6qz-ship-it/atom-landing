What is AI Readiness?

Why AI assistants skip most Shopify stores

When a shopper asks ChatGPT "what's the best minimalist home goods brand?" or Perplexity "where can I buy premium coffee online?" — AI assistants scan thousands of stores and recommend only the ones they can fully understand.

To understand a store, AI needs structured data (schema markup), clear product information, pricing signals, trust indicators like reviews and return policies, and content that explicitly answers shopping questions.

Most Shopify stores were built for human visitors and Google — not for AI. As AI-powered shopping grows, stores that haven't adapted are being systematically skipped.

The AI Readiness Score

Our score (0–100) measures how well a Shopify store is understood by AI systems. It covers six categories: schema markup, content quality, trust signals, technical SEO, pricing visibility, and brand entity strength.

Stores scoring above 70 are regularly recommended by AI assistants. Below 40 means near-invisible to AI-powered search — and that gap represents real, measurable revenue.
