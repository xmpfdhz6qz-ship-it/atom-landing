What the data shows

What we found across the stores we have actually scanned

These figures are computed live from the stores in our database, scored on the 8-factor AI Commerce Score. They update as we scan more stores. No projections, no estimates. Just what the scores show.

Live across our database
Of 61,651 DTC stores scored so far, 13,793 (22%) fall into AI Invisible Risk (under 50/100), and 66 (0%) reach Highly Recommendable (85+). The average score is 55/100.
The most common critical gap is missing or weak structured data, the JSON-LD that lets AI agents read what you sell, at what price, and how trustworthy you are.
Since Shopify began auto-generating agentic files in May 2026, the presence of llms.txt is no longer the differentiator. Quality and store-specificity are.
