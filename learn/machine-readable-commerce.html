Machine-Readable Commerce: built for AI

Machine-Readable Commerce is not a feature. It is an architectural approach. The practice of structuring your store so AI shopping agents can parse, evaluate, and confidently recommend it.

Foundation 🕒 8 min read 📅 May 2026 Atom Foundry
Definition

What is Machine-Readable Commerce?

Machine-Readable Commerce
Machine-Readable Commerce is the practice of structuring product data, trust signals, pricing, and commerce information so AI systems can parse and verify it without human assistance. It is the complete architectural approach to making a store legible to AI agents.

Most ecommerce stores are built for the human eye. AI agents cannot see beautiful design. They see structured signals. A store optimized for human experience but not for machine readability will be invisible to AI shopping agents regardless of how good the product is.

The architecture

Five layers of machine-readable infrastructure

1
Structured Data Layer (Schema Markup)
JSON-LD Product schema with name, description, image, price, currency, availability, and brand. Organization schema. AggregateRating schema. This is the foundation. Without it, AI agents are working from unstructured guesses.
2
Commerce Accuracy Layer
Prices in server-rendered HTML with schema:price and schema:priceCurrency. Stock availability visible. Shipping and return information in crawlable text, not JavaScript modals. Failed by more than 60% of stores.
3
Semantic Content Layer
Specific product category language in H1. Use-case-specific copy that matches how buyers phrase queries. FAQ sections with direct answers. Image alt text with descriptive product attributes.
4
Trust Signal Layer
Review counts and ratings in HTML. Return policy text directly in page HTML. Contact information visible and crawlable. The AI Trust Graph is built from what the internet can verify.
5
AI Navigation Layer
llms.txt with specific brand positioning. Clean robots.txt that allows AI crawlers. Updated sitemap. Internal link structure that guides AI to your most important pages.
Where most stores fail: Layers 2 and 3. Commerce Accuracy failures and Semantic Content gaps account for the majority of AI Commerce Score gaps across the 6,789 stores Atom Foundry has analyzed.
FAQ

Common questions

What is Machine-Readable Commerce?▾
Machine-Readable Commerce is the practice of structuring product data, trust signals, pricing, and commerce information so AI systems can parse and verify it without human assistance.
What are the most important elements of Machine-Readable Commerce?▾
The five layers in order of impact are: structured data (JSON-LD schema), commerce accuracy (prices and policies in server-rendered HTML), semantic content (specific language and FAQ schema), trust signals, and AI navigation (llms.txt and sitemap).
How is Machine-Readable Commerce different from SEO?▾
SEO optimizes for Google crawlers. Machine-Readable Commerce optimizes for AI agents that cannot execute JavaScript, cannot see images, and rely on structured signals to understand what a store sells and why it is trustworthy.
