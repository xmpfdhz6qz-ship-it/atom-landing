Definition

What Agentic Commerce actually means

Agentic Commerce · Definition
Agentic Commerce is the model where AI agents autonomously browse, compare, and complete purchases on behalf of buyers, without requiring human input at each step. The buyer states what they want. The AI agent does everything else.

The word "agentic" comes from AI agent: a system that can take actions, make decisions, and pursue goals autonomously. In commerce, an agentic AI can receive a request like "find me the best eco-friendly moisturizer for sensitive skin under $60 and buy it" and complete that entire flow without the buyer visiting a single website.

This is fundamentally different from AI recommendation, where AI suggests and a human clicks. In agentic commerce, AI completes the purchase. Your store needs to be readable, trustworthy, and transactable by an AI agent that may never show a human your website at all.

The three files that enable Agentic Commerce

Shopify generates three files automatically for every store. These are the files AI agents read when evaluating whether to recommend and purchase from your store. The files exist for every store. Content quality is what separates the stores AI chooses from the ones it skips.

File
What it does
What determines quality
llms.txt
Gives AI agents a description of your store, your brand, your products, and your agent endpoints. The first thing AI reads when evaluating your store.
Product descriptions, brand positioning, pricing clarity, and niche specificity in the content you provide.
llms-full.txt
Full JSON product catalog. Every product, variant, price, and availability signal. Used by AI agents to compare your products against competitors in real time.
The quality of your underlying product data in Shopify. Accurate prices, real stock levels, complete variant information.
agents.md
Checkout instructions for AI agents. Tells an AI agent how to complete a purchase autonomously on behalf of a buyer without a human completing the checkout manually.
Whether your checkout flow is compatible with autonomous agent completion. Requires proper checkout API access and clear instructions.

Having these files is the starting point. AI agents need high-quality content in your llms.txt to recommend you confidently. A file with generic product names and no pricing context gives AI almost nothing to work with.

What you need to get right

What AI agents evaluate in Agentic Commerce

📋
Product data quality
Specific product descriptions, accurate pricing, real availability. AI agents make purchasing decisions based on your llms-full.txt. Incomplete data means wrong recommendations.
🛡️
Machine-readable trust signals
Reviews with AggregateRating schema, return policy in crawlable HTML, SSL, shipping clarity. AI agents will not complete a purchase from a store they cannot verify as trustworthy.
🎯
Niche positioning clarity
AI agents match stores to buyer intent. Generic positioning like "premium quality" gives no intent signal. Specific positioning like "minimalist skincare for sensitive skin" matches precisely to buyer queries.
💳
Checkout compatibility
Your agents.md file needs to correctly describe how an AI agent can complete a checkout. If the checkout flow requires steps the agent cannot complete autonomously, the purchase fails and the agent moves on.
⚡
JSON-LD schema markup
Product, Organization, and AggregateRating schema give AI agents structured data to verify your llms.txt content against your actual website. Stores with schema score significantly higher in agentic evaluations.
🌐
External authority signals
Reddit mentions, YouTube reviews, press coverage, creator endorsements. AI agents cross-reference the broader internet to verify that your store is trustworthy before completing a purchase on someone's behalf.
FAQ

Agentic Commerce common questions

What is Agentic Commerce? ▾
Agentic Commerce is the model where AI agents autonomously browse, compare, and complete purchases on behalf of buyers, without requiring human input at each step. The buyer states what they want. The AI agent finds the best option, evaluates it against their criteria, and completes the purchase. Shopify launched the infrastructure for this model in May 2026 with its Agentic Dashboard.
How does Shopify's Agentic Dashboard work? ▾
Shopify's Agentic Dashboard automatically generates three files for every store: llms.txt (store description and agent endpoints), llms-full.txt (full JSON product catalog), and agents.md (checkout instructions for AI agents). AI agents like Alexa for Shopping, ChatGPT, and Perplexity read these files to understand what the store sells, at what price, and how to complete a purchase autonomously on behalf of a buyer.

How is Agentic Commerce different from regular AI shopping? ▾
Regular AI shopping means AI recommends a product and a human clicks, browses, and buys manually. Agentic Commerce means AI completes the entire flow autonomously on behalf of the buyer. The buyer may never visit your website. The AI agent reads your product data, evaluates your trust signals, and completes the checkout using your agents.md file without any human interaction with your store.
