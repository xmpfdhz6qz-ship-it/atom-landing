FAQ

Human view vs AI view: common questions

Why does my store look fine to me but score low with AI?
Human eyes and AI readers extract information completely differently. You see a photo, a badge, five stars. AI needs that same information in structured, machine-readable form: schema markup, explicit values, crawlable text. A store can be visually polished and structurally invisible at the same time.
What is AI extractability?
AI extractability is how completely an AI system can pull verifiable facts out of a page: price, availability, rating, brand, policies. High extractability means AI can quote your store with confidence. Low extractability means AI has to guess, hedge, or skip the product entirely.
Do I need to redesign my store to fix this?
No. Almost none of this is visual. The fixes are structural: schema markup, server-rendered prices, real alt text, crawlable policy pages. A store can look identical to a human before and after and score completely differently to AI.
How is this different from generic "optimize for AI" content advice?
Most advice describes what to write. This measures what AI can actually see and extract from what already exists. It is the difference between guessing what might help and watching, signal by signal, what does.
