Definition

FAQ

Questions about Prompt Visibility

Is Prompt Visibility the same as Recommendation Share?
They are close. Prompt Visibility is whether you appear across a set of prompts. Share focuses on the high intent buying prompts specifically and how often you win them. Visibility is the broader appearance measure.
How many prompts should I test?
Enough to be representative of your niche, often a few dozen. The exact set matters less than testing real buyer phrasing consistently over time.
Where do I start?
A free AI Commerce Score gives you the signal baseline. From there, prompt testing shows how that translates into actual appearances.
