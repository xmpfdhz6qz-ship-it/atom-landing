What Is It

Not "you're visible." You're the default.

Intent Authority is the strength with which AI associates a brand with a particular buyer intent. A brand with high Intent Authority is not just present for an intent but treated as a default answer to it, appearing early and described with confidence. It is intent-specific: a brand can hold high authority for one intent and none for an adjacent one.

Why AI Cares

Being the authority for "best magnesium for sleep" is worth more than appearing weakly across many intents. Intent Authority concentrates Recommendation Position and Confidence on the exact queries that drive a brand's economics, rather than spreading thin.

Example

For searches like "best fresh dog food delivery," AI consistently names one brand first and describes it in the most detail. That brand holds high Intent Authority for that intent, even if larger competitors appear lower in the same answer.

FAQ

Questions about Intent Authority

What is Intent Authority?
Intent Authority is the strength with which AI associates a brand with a particular buyer intent. A brand with high Intent Authority is not just present for an intent but treated as a default answer to it, appearing early and described with confidence.
How is Intent Authority different from Recommendation Share?
Recommendation Share measures how often you are recommended across a set of prompts. Intent Authority asks a narrower question: when this exact buyer need appears, are you the brand AI reaches for first, without having to search or compare.
Can a brand have high Authority for one intent and none for another?
Yes, and this is the core of the metric. Intent Authority is intent-specific. A brand can be the default answer for one query and be entirely absent from an adjacent one, even within the same category.
Does being a famous brand guarantee Intent Authority?
No. A large, well-known brand can lose a specific intent to a smaller specialist if AI treats the specialist as the more precise answer. Authority beats fame when the intent is specific.
What increases Intent Authority?
Content that repeatedly and specifically answers one buyer question: buying guides, FAQs, reviews, and external mentions that reinforce the same brand-to-intent association until AI treats it as the default.
