FAQ

AI Commerce Score: common questions

What is the AI Commerce Score?▾
The AI Commerce Score is a composite 0 to 100 measurement of how well AI shopping agents can understand, trust, and recommend an ecommerce store. It is calculated across 8 scored factors: Semantic Clarity, AI Readability, AI Trust Confidence, AI Interpretability, Recommendation Confidence, Commerce Accuracy, AI Crawlability, and External Authority Signals.
What is the average AI Commerce Score?▾
Across 6,789 stores scanned by Atom Foundry, the average AI Commerce Score is 45 out of 100. 52% of stores score below 50 and are in AI Visibility Risk. Zero stores currently score above 85. The data reflects how early the AI commerce era is and how much opportunity exists for stores that act now.
How do I improve my AI Commerce Score?▾
The fastest improvements come from: adding Product and Organization JSON-LD schema markup (up to 20 points), ensuring prices are in server-rendered HTML (up to 10 points), enabling AggregateRating schema in your review app (up to 8 points), moving return policy to crawlable HTML (up to 7 points), and customizing llms.txt via Shopify Agentic Dashboard.
Is a higher AI Commerce Score always better?▾
Yes. A higher AI Commerce Score means AI shopping agents can more confidently understand and recommend your store. Stores above 70 appear in AI recommendations more frequently, at better Recommendation Positions, and with stronger language. Stores below 50 are typically excluded from AI recommendation flows entirely.
