Definition
The AI Commerce Score is the composite 0 to 100 measurement of how well AI shopping agents can understand, trust, and recommend a store, built from 8 weighted measurement layers. A score below 50 means AI systems are actively skipping the store. A score above 85 means AI agents can confidently recommend it, and very few stores in the Atom Foundry database have reached that threshold so far.

Why AI cares

AI needs one comparable number before it will consider recommending any merchant at all. Without it, AI has no way to compare a store against competitors, so it defaults to skipping it.

15% weight

Commerce
15% weight

AI agents are starting to complete purchases, so a wrong price or stock status breaks the transaction and the trust that comes with it.

Intent
10% weight

AI agents match stores to specific buyer intent, not keywords. Generic copy cannot match a specific request.

Confidence
10% weight

There is a real difference between a store AI mentions and a store AI trusts enough to recommend first. This factor measures one narrow signal, verifiable social proof, not the store's overall Recommendation Confidence, which is the broader outcome all 8 factors build toward.

→
Authority
5% weight

A brand with no mentions or citations outside its own channels is treated as unverified by AI systems trained on the wider internet.

Why does it matter?

A score below 50 means AI systems are actively skipping the store in recommendation flows. A score above 85 means AI agents can confidently recommend it, and very few stores in the Atom Foundry database have reached that threshold so far.

What improves it?

The fastest gains usually come from the heaviest-weighted layers: AI Structured Signals, Core Technical, AI Trust, and Commerce & Feed Accuracy each carry 15% of the score. Fixing missing schema, a broken trust signal, or an inaccurate price moves the number quickly.

Show me an example.

A beauty brand scored 38. The scan found missing trust signals and JavaScript-rendered prices AI could not read. Fixing both moved the score to 61 and brought the store back into AI recommendation flows.

Show research.

The score is built from 8 measurement layers benchmarked across 66,090 scanned stores, weighted by how much each layer changes AI recommendation behavior. Full methodology and thresholds are published on the Methodology page.
