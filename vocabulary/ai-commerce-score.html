What it is

How AI grades your store

AI Commerce Score
The AI Commerce Score is a 0 to 100 measure of how ready your store is to be discovered, trusted, and recommended by AI shopping agents, built from eight weighted factors that mirror how those agents actually evaluate a merchant.

Search ranked pages. AI recommends merchants. The signals are not the same, and a store can rank well on Google while being effectively invisible to ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Apple Intelligence. The AI Commerce Score measures the new signals: can AI read you, confirm what you sell, verify you are trustworthy, and match you to a buyer. Each factor is weighted by how much it actually moves AI recommendation behavior.

Score composition

Eight factors, 100 points

Five factors carry the heaviest weight at 15 percent each. Two carry 10 percent, and external authority carries 5 percent, a weight we expect to grow as AI training data expands.

The eight factors

What each factor measures

Each factor maps to a specific question an AI agent asks about your store. Several have their own deep-dive guide, linked below.

01
Semantic Visuals & Image Clarity
AI vision reads your product images. Alt text must match what the photo shows, and critical information must not be locked inside graphics AI cannot parse.
15%
02
AI Structured Signals
JSON-LD schema and llms.txt: the primary language AI uses to confirm what you sell, for how much, and who stands behind the store.

15%
04
AI Trust & Transaction Confidence
Machine-readable policies, an agent-navigable checkout, and a clear legal identity AI can verify before routing a buyer to you.
15%
05
Commerce & Feed Accuracy
Price and availability that agree across your HTML, your schema, and your product feed. A mismatch surfaced to a buyer costs AI trust.
15%
06
User Intent Match
How well your content maps to real buyer intent rather than keywords. Generic copy cannot match a specific, contextual request.
10%
07
Recommendation Confidence
AggregateRating schema and parseable review sentiment: the social proof that decides whether AI mentions you or champions you.
10%
08
External Authority Signals
Brand mentions and citations in Reddit, press, and AI indexes. A brand absent everywhere external is treated as unverified.
5%

FAQ

AI Commerce Score: common questions

What is the AI Commerce Score?▾
It is a 0 to 100 measure of how ready your store is to be discovered, trusted, and recommended by AI shopping agents. It is built from eight weighted factors covering visuals, structured data, technical interpretability, trust, commerce accuracy, intent match, recommendation confidence, and external authority. It predicts whether AI includes or excludes your store from its recommendations.
How are the eight factors weighted?▾
Five factors carry 15 percent each: Semantic Visuals, AI Structured Signals, Core Technical and Interpretability, AI Trust and Transaction Confidence, and Commerce and Feed Accuracy. User Intent Match and Recommendation Confidence carry 10 percent each. External Authority Signals carries 5 percent. The weights total 100 points.
What score do I need to be recommended?▾
Below 50, AI agents are systematically excluding your store. From 50 to 69 you are read but recommended weakly. From 70 to 84 you are recommended but stronger signals would lift frequency and position. At 85 and above AI can recommend you with high confidence. The current average across scanned DTC stores is around 45.
How is this different from an SEO score?▾
SEO scores measure how well you rank in a list of links. The AI Commerce Score measures whether an AI agent will recommend you in a conversation. The signals differ: schema, machine-readable trust, feed accuracy, and intent match matter far more to AI than traditional ranking factors, and a store can score well on one and poorly on the other.
