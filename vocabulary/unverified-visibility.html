FAQ

Unverified Visibility: common questions

What is Unverified Visibility?
Unverified Visibility is the state where an AI agent can crawl and read your store but cannot confirm enough about it to recommend it. Your store is technically visible to AI and still absent from its answers.
How is this different from being invisible to AI?
An invisible store fails at the first gate: AI cannot read it at all, and the problem is obvious once measured. An unverified-visible store clears that gate easily, then stalls at trust, fact-confirmation, or intent-matching, which is much harder to notice because everything else about the store looks normal.
How would I know if my store is stuck here?
There is no visible symptom. Traffic, rankings, and checkout all work normally. The only way to see it is to measure your AI Commerce Score and Recommendation Share directly, since Unverified Visibility does not produce an error, only an absence.
Is this the same thing as AI Trust Confidence?
They're related but not the same question. AI Trust Confidence measures whether AI can verify your store is trustworthy, one factor. Unverified Visibility describes the broader failure mode of stalling anywhere between being read and being recommended, which can be caused by trust, missing structured data, or generic positioning.
What is the fastest way out?
Start with the signals that block verification entirely: AggregateRating and Organization schema, plus a crawlable return policy. These typically move a store out of the unverified band faster than adding more content or traffic ever would.
