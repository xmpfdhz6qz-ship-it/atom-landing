What is llms.txt

The file AI agents read before your homepage

llms.txt
llms.txt is a plain text file at the root of your domain that tells AI language models what your store sells, how it is structured, and what content they are permitted to use.

Think of it as a robots.txt for AI agents, but instead of access rules, it contains product information, brand positioning, and navigation guidance. When ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Alexa for Shopping evaluates your store, this is often the first file they read.

FAQ

llms.txt: common questions

Does my Shopify store already have llms.txt?
Yes. Shopify's Agentic Dashboard launched in May 2026 and automatically generates llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and agents.md for every Shopify store. You can view your file at yourstore.com/llms.txt. The question is not whether you have it, the question is whether the content is optimized.
What does llms.txt actually do?
It gives AI agents a concise, machine-facing summary of your store before they crawl anything else: what you sell, who it's for, and what content they're permitted to use. It shapes the very first impression an AI forms of your business.
What is the difference between llms.txt and llms-full.txt?
llms.txt is a concise summary: brand positioning, key categories, trust summary, and navigation guidance. llms-full.txt is the complete version with your full product catalog, detailed descriptions, and comprehensive inventory data. AI agents typically read llms.txt first, then use llms-full.txt to browse your full catalog.
What is the difference between llms.txt and agents.md?
llms.txt and llms-full.txt describe who you are and what you sell. agents.md is different: it tells autonomous AI agents how they're allowed to act on your store, including checkout steps, permissions, and constraints for AI-initiated purchases.
Does llms.txt replace schema markup?
No. llms.txt and JSON-LD schema markup serve different purposes. llms.txt gives AI agents a high-level overview of your store before crawling. Schema markup gives AI agents machine-readable structured data within individual pages. Both are required for strong AI Readiness.
How should I optimize my llms.txt?
Go to Shopify Admin, Settings, Agentic. Replace the generic auto-generated description with specific positioning: who you are, what you sell, price range, and what makes you different. Add category depth with price ranges, a trust summary, and explicit niche signals.
Can AI agents actually use llms.txt today?
Yes. Since Shopify's Agentic Dashboard launched in May 2026, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI shopping systems read llms.txt as part of evaluating a store. It's already live infrastructure, not a future standard.
