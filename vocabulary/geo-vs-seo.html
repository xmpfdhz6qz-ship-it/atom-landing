The relationship

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO and SEO are complementary. SEO drives discovery through Google search. GEO drives recommendations through AI shopping agents. Both channels matter in 2026 and both are growing.

The practical question is not SEO or GEO. It is what you do first. For most stores, GEO improvements are faster and cheaper than equivalent SEO gains. Adding schema markup, moving prices to HTML, and customizing llms.txt can be done in a day. Equivalent SEO improvements through content and link building take months.

Both
Meta titles with specific intent language
Meta titles help Google rankings. AI also reads them for initial context. Specific, niche-aligned meta titles help both channels. Generic titles help neither.

JSON-LD schema markup (Product and Organization)
Helpful for SEO rich snippets but critical for AI recommendation probability. This is the single highest-impact GEO action for most stores. Zero schema = AI guesses.

Price in server-rendered HTML
Irrelevant for SEO. Critical for GEO. AI agents cannot read JavaScript-rendered prices. More than 60% of stores fail this check.

llms.txt file via Shopify Agentic Dashboard
Irrelevant for SEO. Critical for GEO. Gives AI agents explicit structured information about your brand, products, and positioning. 30 minutes to set up.

Helpful for SEO but critical for GEO. Copy that matches how buyers phrase queries to AI determines whether AI matches your store to buyer intent.

Google's most important ranking signals. Irrelevant for AI recommendation probability. Essential for Google visibility but have no direct GEO impact.

FAQ

GEO vs SEO: common questions

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?▾
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) optimizes for ranking in Google search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes for being recommended by AI shopping systems like ChatGPT, Alexa for Shopping, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity. SEO targets keyword rankings and backlinks. GEO targets structured data, trust signals, semantic clarity, and recommendation confidence.
Does GEO replace SEO?▾
No. GEO and SEO are complementary. SEO drives traffic from Google search. GEO drives recommendations from AI shopping agents. As AI-referred orders grow 13x year-over-year, GEO becomes increasingly important alongside SEO. Stores that optimize for both capture traffic from both traditional search and the growing AI recommendation layer.
Can a store rank well on Google but be invisible to AI?▾
Yes. A store can rank number one on Google for competitive keywords and still score below 50 on the AI Commerce Score, placing it in AI Visibility Risk. The signals Google uses to rank pages (keywords, backlinks, page authority) are almost entirely different from the signals AI agents use to evaluate and recommend stores (structured data, trust signals, semantic clarity, commerce accuracy).
What does GEO require that SEO does not?▾
GEO requires: JSON-LD Product and Organization schema markup, prices in server-rendered HTML not JavaScript, return policy in crawlable text not a modal, AggregateRating schema from reviews, llms.txt via Shopify Agentic Dashboard, specific niche positioning language, and external authority signals. None of these significantly affect Google rankings but all directly affect AI recommendation probability.

Is GEO the same as AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?▾
AEO typically refers to optimizing for AI answer engines in a general content context, like getting cited in AI-generated answers to informational queries. GEO for ecommerce is more specific: optimizing for AI shopping recommendation flows where buyers are actively looking to purchase. GEO requires commerce-specific signals like product schema, price accuracy, and trust signals that go beyond what AEO typically covers.
