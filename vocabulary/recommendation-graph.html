FAQ

Questions about the Recommendation Graph

Can I be in more than one cluster?
Yes, and strong presence across several relevant clusters is exactly what high Recommendation Density looks like. The key is that the clusters fit your real brand.
How does AI decide my cluster?
From your signals: what your content says, what your schema declares, and what the wider web associates you with. Consistent signals place you firmly. Mixed signals scatter you.
