AI Retrieval Signals: the 8 factors

AI shopping agents evaluate every store across 8 scored factors before making a recommendation. Understanding each factor and what to fix first is the technical foundation of AI Commerce Intelligence.

Technical 🕒 9 min read 📅 May 2026 Atom Foundry
The framework

How the AI Commerce Score is built

The AI Commerce Score is a composite 0 to 100 measurement across 8 factors. Each measures a specific dimension of how well AI shopping agents can understand, trust, and recommend a store.

Industry benchmark: Across 6,789 scanned stores, the average AI Commerce Score is 45 out of 100. Zero stores score above 85. 52% score below 50. Most ecommerce infrastructure was built for humans and Google, not for AI recommendation systems.
The 8 factors

Each factor explained

1. Semantic Clarity (0 to 18 points)

Can AI understand what this store sells, who it serves, and why it is different? Measures hero specificity, USP clarity, and description quality. "Premium quality products" scores zero. "Organic skincare for sensitive skin" scores high.

2. AI Readability (0 to 20 points)

Can AI extract content cleanly? Measures heading hierarchy, FAQ structure, server-side rendering, and semantic formatting.

3. AI Trust Confidence (0 to 18 points)

Machine-readable trust signals: reviews with star ratings in HTML, return policy text, shipping info, SSL, and contact information.

4. AI Interpretability (0 to 12 points)

Does AI understand what the store actually offers? Use-case understanding and context comprehension. "Wellness products" is ambiguous. "Collagen supplements for joint health" is interpretable.

5. Recommendation Confidence (0 to 14 points)

Delivery info visibility, return process clarity, and "what you see is what you get" signals. Subscription traps reduce this significantly.

6. Commerce Accuracy (0 to 10 points)

Price visible in HTML without JavaScript, schema price and priceCurrency present, stock availability visible. Failed by more than 60% of stores.

7. AI Crawlability (0 to 12 points)

Robots.txt, sitemap, mobile viewport, clean hierarchy, no JavaScript-only content blocks.

8. External Authority Signals (0 to 3 points)

About page present, social links in HTML, founder mentioned, consistent brand name. Small in technical scoring but large impact on overall recommendation probability through AI Trust Graph evaluation.

Where to start: Commerce Accuracy and AI Trust Confidence fail in more than 70% of stores. Fixing prices in HTML and moving return policy into server-rendered HTML are the two highest-impact actions for most stores.
FAQ

Common questions

What are the 8 AI retrieval signals?▾
The 8 factors in the AI Commerce Score are: Semantic Clarity (0-18), AI Readability (0-20), AI Trust Confidence (0-18), AI Interpretability (0-12), Recommendation Confidence (0-14), Commerce Accuracy (0-10), AI Crawlability (0-12), and External Authority Signals (0-3). Maximum score is 100.
Which AI retrieval signal matters most?▾
Commerce Accuracy and AI Trust Confidence fail most often across scanned stores. Fixing prices in HTML and making return policy server-rendered typically provide the fastest score improvement.
How is the AI Commerce Score calculated?▾
The AI Commerce Score is the sum of all 8 factor scores, maximum 100. Above 85 is Highly Recommendable. Between 70 and 84 is Moderately Recommendable. Between 50 and 69 is Low Recommendation Confidence. Below 50 is AI Visibility Risk.
