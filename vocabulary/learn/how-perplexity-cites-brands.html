How Perplexity cites brands.

Perplexity does not remember your store. It retrieves it. Perplexity searches the live web when a buyer asks a question, decides which sources to retrieve, extract, and cite, and that makes it fundamentally different from memory driven AI systems like ChatGPT.

The fundamental difference

Perplexity is a retrieval system.

ChatGPT answers from what it learned during training, refreshed periodically. Perplexity answers from what it finds on the web right now. Same question, two completely different paths to an answer.

Getting crawled is not the same as getting cited.

That is the line between AI crawlability and AI citation visibility. A page can be fully accessible to Perplexity and still never make it past stage three.

Worked example

Why one page gets cited and another does not.

Same product category, same query, two different outcomes. This shows directional signal strength, not a guarantee. Perplexity does not publish its exact weighting, so treat this as stronger retrieval signals versus weaker ones, not a promise of citation.

Store A
Product page: premium collagen supplement
Generic positioning, no specific buyer intent
Weak answer structure, no direct FAQ
Little contextual evidence on the page
Weak external references around the brand
Citation probability: low
Store B
Product page: hydrolyzed collagen for endurance athletes
Specific buyer intent named directly in the copy
FAQ block: is collagen useful for runners, answered plainly
Evidence linked: independent review, specialist article
External mentions reinforcing the same claim
Citation probability: higher
The important distinction

Crawlable is not retrievable is not citable.

These are three separate hurdles, not one. Passing the first tells you almost nothing about the other two.

Crawlable
AI can access the page at all.
↓
Retrievable
AI can find the page for a specific query.
↓
Citable
AI can extract useful evidence and use the page as a source.
A page can pass the first layer and fail the next two.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does Perplexity decide which brands to cite?
Perplexity crawls in real time and evaluates page relevance to the query, external links pointing to the page, content freshness, and how easily it can extract structured answers.
Is Perplexity optimization different from ChatGPT optimization?
Yes. ChatGPT uses training data and evaluates brand entities. Perplexity crawls in real time and cites sources directly. Improving Perplexity visibility requires specific query-answering content and current external citations.
Can I see if Perplexity is crawling my store?
Yes. Check your server logs for the PerplexityBot user agent. You can also add Perplexity crawl rules to your llms.txt file.
