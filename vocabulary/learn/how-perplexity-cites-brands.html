How Perplexity cites brands

Perplexity is the AI system most different from ChatGPT. It crawls the web in real time and cites sources directly. That changes everything about how to optimize for it.

Technical 🕒 5 min read 📅 May 2026 Atom Foundry
The key difference

Perplexity crawls. ChatGPT retrieves.

ChatGPT relies on training data. Its knowledge of brands was formed during training, often months before a buyer asks. Perplexity crawls the web in real time when a buyer submits a query. It finds current pages, reads them, extracts answers, and cites the sources it used.

ChatGPT
Uses training data (months old)
Entity-based evaluation
Reddit and press matter most
Cannot be updated in real time
Perplexity
✓ Real-time web crawl
✓ Citation-based responses
✓ External links matter heavily
✓ Content updates reflect immediately
How to optimize

Three actions that improve Perplexity visibility

1
Create highly specific, query-answering content
Perplexity finds pages that directly answer the buyer query. Create FAQ sections that answer the exact questions buyers ask. "Is collagen good for runners?" as a FAQ heading with a direct answer is exactly what Perplexity finds and cites.
2
Build external citations and links
Perplexity follows external links and weights pages with strong external citation. Press coverage, blog mentions, and review platform pages that link to your store feed Perplexity crawl.
3
Ensure pages are server-rendered and fast
Perplexity crawls in real time. Slow, JavaScript-heavy, or poorly structured pages get extracted less reliably. Server-side rendering directly improves Perplexity extractability.
FAQ

Common questions

How does Perplexity decide which brands to cite?▾
Perplexity crawls in real time and evaluates page relevance to the query, external links pointing to the page, content freshness, and how easily it can extract structured answers.
Is Perplexity optimization different from ChatGPT optimization?▾
Yes. ChatGPT uses training data and evaluates brand entities. Perplexity crawls in real time and cites sources directly. Improving Perplexity visibility requires specific query-answering content and current external citations.
Can I see if Perplexity is crawling my store?▾
Yes. Check your server logs for the PerplexityBot user agent. You can also add Perplexity crawl rules to your llms.txt file.
