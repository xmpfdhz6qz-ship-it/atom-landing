This is exactly why Prompt Visibility has to be measured prompt by prompt, and why Recommendation Share is measured across a whole set of buyer prompts, not a single query.

Known versus recommendable

Being known is not being recommended.

Unknown
AI has insufficient evidence about your brand.
Known
AI knows your brand but lacks enough confidence for the query.
Recommendable
AI has enough evidence and relevance to confidently include you.

Most stores that check their own name in ChatGPT are only testing whether they are known. See Unverified Visibility for what happens in that gap between known and recommendable.

What to do about it

How to increase your recommendation probability.

01
Strengthen the entity
Organization, then brand, then products, then category. AI needs all four connected clearly before it can recommend with confidence.
02
Increase external evidence
Reviews, mentions, Reddit, press. AI trusts what other sources say about you more than what you say about yourself.
03
Sharpen intent
Say exactly what you sell + who it's for + use case + differentiator. Generic positioning matches nothing well.
04
Make facts extractable
Product schema, price, availability, policies. If AI cannot verify a fact, it will not use it to justify a recommendation.
05
Measure recommendations
Build a prompt set, run it repeatedly, and track Recommendation Share, Recommendation Position, and Recommendation Confidence over time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does ChatGPT decide which brands to recommend?
ChatGPT evaluates brands through entity recognition, trust verification using Reddit and press data, intent matching to score how well each brand fits the buyer query, and confidence scoring. Brands that score well on all four get recommended.
Does ChatGPT Shopping work differently from regular ChatGPT?
ChatGPT Shopping adds a paid product feed layer. But the underlying organic recommendation engine works the same way. Brand entity, trust signals, and intent matching still determine organic ChatGPT recommendations.
How do I get my store recommended by ChatGPT?
Build brand entity through Reddit presence and press coverage, add Organization schema, make positioning specific and buyer-intent-aligned, and get reviews on Trustpilot or Google.
