What is llms.txt

The file AI agents read before your homepage

llms.txt
llms.txt is a plain text file at the root of your domain that tells AI language models what your store sells, how it is structured, and what content they are permitted to use. AI agents read it before crawling individual pages — it is the first impression your store makes on AI.

Think of llms.txt as a robots.txt for AI agents — but instead of access rules, it contains product information, brand positioning, and navigation guidance. When Alexa for Shopping, ChatGPT, or Perplexity evaluates your store, llms.txt is often the first file they read.

Shopify's Agentic Dashboard generates three files automatically: llms.txt (summary), llms-full.txt (complete product catalog), and agents.md (checkout instructions for autonomous AI purchases). All three affect your AI recommendation probability.

Why default llms.txt is not enough

What Shopify generates vs what actually works

✗
Generic brand description
Shopify auto-generates a minimal brand description from your store name and category. This gives AI agents almost no signal for matching your store to specific buyer queries. Needs: specific positioning, target customer, price range, and differentiators.
✗
No product category depth
Default llms.txt lists top-level categories only. AI agents need subcategory structure, product descriptions, and price ranges to match your inventory to specific buyer intents.
✗
Missing trust signals
Default llms.txt contains no information about reviews, return policy, shipping speed, or trust credentials. AI agents use llms.txt as a trust shortcut — empty files score lower.
✗
No niche positioning
Without explicit niche signals in llms.txt — luxury, eco, minimalist, premium, budget, handmade — AI agents cannot match your store to buyers with specific intent queries.
How to optimize your llms.txt

What to add to your Shopify llms.txt

1
Open Shopify Admin → Settings → Agentic
Find your current llms.txt content. This is what every AI agent reads when they first evaluate your store. It is almost certainly minimal and needs enriching.
2
Add a specific brand positioning statement
Replace the generic description with a specific one: who you are, what you sell, to whom, at what price range, and what makes you different. Example: "Premium sustainable activewear for women, $60-$180, organic cotton and recycled materials, free shipping over $100."
3
Add product categories with price ranges
List your main product categories with typical price ranges. This helps AI agents match your inventory to budget-specific buyer queries without crawling every product page.
4
Add trust summary
Include: return policy summary, shipping speed, review count and rating, and years in business. AI agents use this to verify trustworthiness before recommending your store.
5
Add niche and intent signals
Explicitly state your niche modifiers: luxury, eco, minimalist, handmade, vegan, premium, budget-friendly, clinical-grade, etc. These signals directly improve your match rate for specific buyer queries.
FAQ

llms.txt questions

Does my Shopify store already have llms.txt?▾
Yes. Shopify's Agentic Dashboard launched in May 2026 and automatically generates llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and agents.md for every Shopify store. You can view your file at yourstore.com/llms.txt. The question is not whether you have it — the question is whether the content is optimized.
How do I edit my Shopify llms.txt?▾
Go to Shopify Admin → Settings → Agentic. You can edit the llms.txt content directly from this panel. Changes take effect within minutes. The Agentic Dashboard also lets you edit llms-full.txt and agents.md.
What is the difference between llms.txt and llms-full.txt?▾
llms.txt is a concise summary of your store — brand positioning, key categories, trust summary, and navigation guidance. llms-full.txt is the complete version with your full product catalog, detailed descriptions, and comprehensive inventory data. AI agents typically read llms.txt first, then use llms-full.txt to browse your full catalog.
Does llms.txt replace schema markup?▾
No. llms.txt and JSON-LD schema markup serve different purposes. llms.txt gives AI agents a high-level overview of your store before crawling. Schema markup gives AI agents machine-readable structured data within individual pages. Both are required for strong AI Readiness. llms.txt without schema = incomplete. Schema without llms.txt = incomplete.
