Recommendation Share™

Paid Audit Monitoring Category: Key Metric Part of: AI Commerce Intelligence
Definition
Recommendation Share is the percentage of high-intent buying prompts relevant to a brand's niche in which that brand appears in AI recommendations. If a brand appears in 18 out of 100 tested buyer prompts, its Recommendation Share is 18%.
Why it matters

The AI-era replacement for keyword rankings

In the SEO era, you tracked keyword rankings. In the AI commerce era, you track Recommendation Share. The shift from position to presence, from ranking to recommendation, changes what you optimize for and how you measure commercial success.

Every buyer prompt that AI answers without mentioning your brand is a potential sale going to a competitor. Recommendation Share quantifies exactly how much of your category's AI traffic you are capturing versus missing. It is the most commercially direct metric in AI Commerce Intelligence.

Related concept

Recommendation Share vs Recommendation Position

Recommendation Share measures how often you appear. Recommendation Position measures where you appear and how confidently AI endorses you when it does. Both dimensions matter for commercial outcomes.

The key insight: A brand with 60% Recommendation Share but always as a weak citation drives less revenue than a brand with 35% Recommendation Share that consistently appears as the first confident recommendation. Share and Position together determine commercial outcomes.
Example

Recommendation Share in practice

A running shoe brand tests 50 buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode. Results:

It appears in 9 prompts out of 50 total. Its Recommendation Share is 18%. It appears as the first recommendation in 4 of those 9 appearances and as a supporting mention in 5. Competitors A and B appear in 32 and 28 prompts respectively, capturing the majority of the niche's AI visibility.

The test reveals an Intent Misalignment problem: the brand appears in "best running shoes" prompts but is missing from "best shoes for flat feet," "marathon training gear," and "injury prevention footwear" prompts despite selling products directly relevant to those queries. Fixing the semantic positioning for those use cases would likely increase Recommendation Share from 18% to above 30% within one to two months.
