Definition
The AI Trust Layer is the verification gate an AI system runs before it will recommend a merchant at all. It checks whether a store's identity, policies, reviews, and public presence can be independently confirmed as evidence. Unlike a ranking factor that raises or lowers position, the Trust Layer is binary: a store either passes the gate and becomes eligible for recommendation, or it does not.
Two different processes

Trust isn't earned here.
It's verified.

A human forms trust in a glance. An AI system cannot glance. It reads evidence, one piece at a time, and it only trusts what it can independently confirm.
