Definition
Intent Misalignment describes a store that is credible and technically solid, but whose positioning, copy, and content are misaligned with how buyers actually ask AI for recommendations in that category. The fix is content strategy, not technical optimization.

FAQ

Questions about Intent Misalignment

How is this different from low Semantic Clarity?
Clarity is about whether AI can understand you at all. Intent Misalignment is when AI understands you fine, but what you are about does not match what buyers are asking for.
Can a strong brand still suffer from it?
Yes, often. Established brands frequently speak in broad brand language while buyers ask in specific use case terms. The mismatch costs them queries they could easily win.
