How ChatGPT Shopping works

What ChatGPT evaluates when recommending stores

When a buyer asks ChatGPT "find me a minimalist leather wallet under $100," ChatGPT does not search Google. It evaluates stores it has indexed based on structured product data, semantic clarity, and trust signals. The store it can most confidently match to the buyer's intent gets recommended.

🚧
Product Schema
ChatGPT reads Product JSON-LD schema to understand what you sell, at what price, and with what specifications. Missing schema means ChatGPT cannot extract product details.
🎯
Semantic Positioning
ChatGPT matches buyer queries to store positioning. "Minimalist leather wallet" only matches stores that explicitly communicate minimalist positioning. Generic stores are invisible.
🛡
Trust Signals
ChatGPT verifies store trustworthiness via machine-readable signals: AggregateRating schema, return policy text, SSL, and contact information. Visual trust does not count.
💰
Commerce Accuracy
Price must be visible in HTML without JavaScript. Stock availability must be readable. Stores with JS-rendered prices are excluded from ChatGPT price comparisons.
How to get recommended by ChatGPT

The fastest fixes for ChatGPT Shopping visibility

1
Add Product and Organization JSON-LD schema
ChatGPT uses structured data as its primary signal. Add @type: Product with name, description, image, price, priceCurrency, availability, and brand. Add @type: Organization with your brand name, logo, and sameAs social links.
2
Sharpen your niche positioning
ChatGPT matches buyer queries to store positioning. Rewrite your homepage hero to explicitly communicate your niche, target customer, price tier, and differentiators. "Premium sustainable yoga wear for women" beats "Quality sportswear" every time.
3
Add AggregateRating schema to reviews
Enable JSON-LD output in your review app. ChatGPT heavily weights review signals when comparing stores. AggregateRating schema with ratingValue and reviewCount is required for ChatGPT to read your reviews.
4
Make prices and stock visible in HTML
If your price only appears after JavaScript runs, ChatGPT cannot read it and cannot include you in price comparison queries. Test: view page source and search for your price. If you cannot find it, ChatGPT cannot either.
5
Optimize your llms.txt content
ChatGPT reads llms.txt to understand your store before crawling individual pages. Shopify auto-generates it but enriching it with specific product descriptions, brand story, and positioning signals significantly improves ChatGPT visibility.
FAQ

ChatGPT Shopping questions

Can ChatGPT recommend products from my Shopify store?▾
Yes. ChatGPT Shopping recommends products from ecommerce stores in response to buyer queries. Whether your store gets recommended depends on how well ChatGPT can read your product data, trust signals, and semantic positioning. Stores without structured data and proper trust signals are systematically excluded.
Does ChatGPT Shopping require a paid integration?▾
ChatGPT Shopping includes both organic recommendations (based on store quality signals) and paid product feed placements. Organic recommendations are available to any store that meets ChatGPT's quality thresholds. Improving your AI Readiness Score is the most effective way to get included in organic ChatGPT recommendations.
How is ChatGPT Shopping different from Google Shopping?▾
Google Shopping ranks stores based on keyword relevance, bid amounts, and product feed quality. ChatGPT Shopping evaluates stores based on semantic intent matching, structured data quality, trust confidence, and brand entity signals. A store can rank well in Google Shopping and still be invisible to ChatGPT Shopping.
How do I know if ChatGPT is recommending my store?▾
Check your analytics for referral traffic from chat.openai.com. You can also run an AI Readiness Score scan at atomfoundry.dev which shows your current ChatGPT visibility gaps and the exact fixes needed to improve your recommendation probability.
