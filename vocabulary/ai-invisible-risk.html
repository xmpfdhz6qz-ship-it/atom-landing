Questions about AI Invisible Risk

How many stores are in this zone?
Across the stores Atom Foundry has scanned, a large share score below 50. It is the most common position, not a rare failure, which is why getting out of it is such an advantage.
Does a low score mean my products are bad?
No. The score measures how readable and trustworthy you are to AI, not product quality. Excellent products with poor structure routinely land in this zone.
How fast can I get out?
It depends on the blockers, but because most are structural fixes rather than slow brand building, many stores can clear the threshold faster than they expect.
