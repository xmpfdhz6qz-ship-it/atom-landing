FAQ

AI Trust Confidence: common questions

What is AI Trust Confidence?
AI Trust Confidence is the degree to which an AI shopping agent can independently verify that your store is legitimate, reliable, and safe to transact with, using structured, machine-readable signals rather than visual cues. It is one of the 8 factors in the AI Commerce Score, weighted at 15%.
Why does trust gate recommendations instead of just ranking them?
Recommending a store is a risk for an AI agent. If it sends a buyer to a store that turns out to be unreliable, the agent looks unreliable too. So trust is checked before scoring, not blended into it: a store that cannot be verified is excluded rather than ranked lower.
Can a store with great reviews still fail AI Trust Confidence?
Yes, and this is the most common failure mode. If reviews are rendered by a JavaScript widget with no AggregateRating schema, an AI agent sees no rating at all, regardless of how many five-star reviews exist for human visitors.
What is the single fastest fix?
Enabling AggregateRating schema in your review app and publishing your return policy as a plain, crawlable page. Together they cover the two heaviest-weighted trust signals, and for most stores both can be live within an afternoon.
Does AI Trust Confidence replace real trust or reviews?
No. It does not create trust, it exposes trust that already exists to a reader that cannot see badges, photos, or brand voice. The fixes are almost entirely structural, not cosmetic.
