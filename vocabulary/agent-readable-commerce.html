What Is It

The infrastructure the agentic era requires.

Agent-Readable Commerce is the next layer beyond Machine-Readable Commerce. Where Machine-Readable Commerce ensures AI can parse and understand a store, Agent-Readable Commerce ensures autonomous AI agents can navigate the entire commerce flow end to end, from product discovery through price verification through checkout completion. It is the infrastructure requirement of the agentic commerce era.

Why AI Cares

Shopify launched Agentic Storefronts in May 2026. AI agents now complete purchases autonomously. Stores that are not agent-readable will be bypassed in the growing share of AI-completed transactions. This is not a future trend. It is already happening.

Example

An agent-readable store has llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and agents.md. Cart and checkout are accessible without JavaScript popups. Prices and availability are in HTML. Returns and shipping are in crawlable text. An agent can complete a purchase without any human input.

FAQ

Questions about Agent-Readable Commerce

What is Agent-Readable Commerce?
Agent-Readable Commerce is e-commerce infrastructure specifically structured for autonomous AI shopping agents that browse, compare, and complete purchases without human input. It is the next layer beyond Machine-Readable Commerce.
How is Agent-Readable Commerce different from Machine-Readable Commerce?
Machine-Readable Commerce ensures AI can parse and understand a store. Agent-Readable Commerce ensures an autonomous agent can navigate the entire commerce flow end to end, from discovery through price verification through checkout completion, without a human present.
Is this actually happening yet?
Yes. Shopify launched Agentic Storefronts in May 2026. AI agents already complete purchases autonomously on supporting stores. This is not a future trend, it is a growing share of transactions today.
What breaks an agent's ability to purchase?
Popups that require a click, prices rendered only in JavaScript, shipping costs hidden until a late step, returns policies that exist only as images, and the absence of a checkout API all stop an agent mid-purchase.
What does an agent-readable store actually have?
An llms.txt and llms-full.txt file, an agents.md file, cart and checkout accessible without JavaScript popups, prices and availability present in HTML, and returns and shipping written in crawlable text.
