FAQ

AI Interpretability: common questions

What is AI Interpretability?▾
AI Interpretability measures whether AI shopping agents can accurately understand what an ecommerce store sells, who it is for, and what problems it solves. It is scored from 0 to 12 points in the AI Commerce Score. A store that AI cannot correctly interpret cannot be recommended for the right buyer queries even if all other signals are strong.
What causes low AI Interpretability?▾
Low AI Interpretability is usually caused by: ambiguous product category language, missing use-case descriptions, too much focus on brand story versus product specifics, no clear target customer in the copy, and product descriptions that describe how something looks rather than what it does and who it helps.
How do I improve AI Interpretability?▾
The highest-impact improvements are: add explicit product category to H1 and meta description, write use-case descriptions for every product category page, include target customer language in positioning, describe what the product does and who it helps rather than just what it is, and add FAQ sections that answer the specific questions buyers ask AI about your category.
Is AI Interpretability the same as Semantic Clarity?▾
They are related but measure different things. Semantic Clarity measures whether your positioning language contains specific buyer-intent signals. AI Interpretability measures whether AI can build an accurate understanding of your product category, target customer, and use cases. A store can have specific language (high Semantic Clarity) but still have low Interpretability if the overall picture AI builds is incomplete or contradictory.
