What Is It

The AI-era version of search volume.

Recommendation Demand is the volume of buyer intent directed at a category or specific intent cluster through AI systems. It is the AI-era equivalent of search volume: how much buyer attention actually flows through a given set of prompts. High Recommendation Demand intents are where recommendation visibility is most commercially valuable.

Why AI Cares

Not all intents are worth the same. Winning a high-demand intent like "best protein powder" matters more than dominating a niche one. Recommendation Demand tells a brand where to concentrate by sizing the buyer attention behind each part of the Buyer Intent Graph.

Example

"Best running shoes" carries far higher Recommendation Demand than "best shoes for plantar fasciitis in wide sizes." A brand prioritizes coverage of high-demand intents first, then expands into the long tail.

FAQ

Questions about Recommendation Demand

What is Recommendation Demand?
Recommendation Demand is the volume of buyer intent directed at a category or specific intent cluster through AI systems. It is the AI-era equivalent of search volume: how much buyer attention actually flows through a given set of prompts.
How is Recommendation Demand different from Recommendation Share?
Recommendation Share measures how often you win a set of prompts. Recommendation Demand measures how large that set of prompts actually is. A brand can have a strong Share of a tiny intent and still capture almost no real buyer attention.
How is Recommendation Demand different from Buyer Intent Coverage?
Coverage measures how much of a category's intents a brand reaches at all. Demand measures how much buyer attention each of those intents actually carries. Coverage is breadth; Demand is where that breadth is worth having.
Why does a high-demand intent matter more than several low-demand ones?
Winning one high-demand intent can route more buyer attention to a brand than dominating ten low-demand ones combined. Demand tells a brand where the buyer attention actually is before it decides where to compete.
Is Recommendation Demand fixed over time?
No. Demand shifts with season, trend cycles, and category news, the same way search volume does. An intent with low demand in January can spike months later.
