Core Concept · Trust Layer

AI Trust Graph.

AI is asking the internet about you. Every confirming source that answers becomes a node in the network that decides whether AI trusts your brand.

Category Core ConceptMaps External validationRelated AI Trust Layer
AI · Trust Established
Definition
The AI Trust Graph is the network of external signals AI uses to validate a brand. Reddit mentions, press coverage, review platform presence, and citations in articles all count. AI does not simply trust what a brand claims about itself, it asks the wider internet to confirm it.
Not a self-report

AI doesn't ask you.
It asks the internet.

AI does not take a brand's word for it. It puts the same question to every external source it can reach, and waits for the internet to answer.
