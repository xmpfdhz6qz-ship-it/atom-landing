The Gap

The same store. Two completely different experiences.

A human visitorAn AI agent
Sees a hero image and reads the headline
Needs structured data to know what the page is even about
Trusts a five star badge on sight
Needs Review and AggregateRating schema to verify the rating exists
Understands "free shipping over $50" instantly
Needs that claim in machine readable text, not just a banner graphic
Forms an impression in seconds
Extracts facts, checks them against other sources, then decides

Passing the human test and passing the AI test are two different exams. Most stores have only ever studied for one of them.

How One Gap Actually Costs You

It is never just "missing schema." It is a chain.

Every failure below follows the same causal chain: technical problem → AI interpretation → recommendation consequence → commercial impact. That chain is what the audit is actually measuring. Click a signal to see its chain.

Signal
Price is available in machine readable HTML.
Failure
Price only appears after JavaScript execution.
AI consequence
The product becomes difficult to compare against alternatives.
Business consequence
Potential exclusion from price sensitive recommendations.
Fix
Expose price and currency in crawlable commerce markup.
Signal
Reviews schema confirms a verifiable star rating and sentiment.
Failure
The five star badge is a styled image with no underlying schema.
AI consequence
AI scores Recommendation Confidence low, regardless of how good the real reviews are.
Business consequence
The merchant loses a social proof signal that competitors with proper schema keep.
Fix
Add Review and AggregateRating schema tied to real review data.
Signal
Stock status is readable as structured data.
Failure
Availability only shows as a colored dot rendered client side.
AI consequence
AI cannot confirm whether the product can actually be purchased right now.
Business consequence
The product is dropped from urgency or availability based recommendations.
Fix
Expose availability status in schema, not just visual styling.

What This Is Not

An AI Commerce Audit is not an SEO audit.

SEO asks whether search engines can find you. AI Commerce asks whether AI systems can understand, trust, compare, and recommend you.

A short clarification, since it is the most common confusion: GEO optimizes content for generative visibility, being mentioned in an AI-generated answer. An AI Commerce Audit measures the underlying commerce signals AI systems use to understand and evaluate a merchant, whether or not that merchant is currently being mentioned at all. You can rank well in GEO and still fail commerce evaluation, and vice versa.

FAQ

AI Commerce Audit questions.

How long does the AI Commerce Audit take?
The full AI Commerce Audit completes in 10 seconds. Enter your domain URL, and the scan analyzes your store across all 8 factors in real time. No waiting, no email required, results displayed immediately.
Is the AI Commerce Audit really free?
Yes. The full AI Commerce Score audit is free, requires no signup, and no credit card. You get your complete score across all 8 factors, the factor breakdown, and your top priority fixes at no cost.
How is an AI Commerce Audit different from an SEO audit?
An SEO audit analyzes keyword rankings, backlinks, page speed, and content for search engine optimization. An AI Commerce Audit analyzes the signals AI shopping agents use to evaluate and recommend stores. A store can pass an SEO audit and fail an AI Commerce Audit completely.
How often should I run an AI Commerce Audit?
Run an audit any time you make significant changes to your store: new theme, new apps, updated product pages, or policy changes. Also run one after implementing fixes from a previous audit to verify improvements. AI agents re-crawl stores frequently, so improvements show up in your score within days.
Does a high AI Commerce Score guarantee AI recommendations?
No. The score measures the merchant's readiness and machine readable signals. Recommendation depends on additional factors including query intent, retrieval, authority, competition, and the AI system itself.
Can a store have a high SEO score but a low AI Commerce Score?
Yes. A store can be technically strong for traditional search while exposing insufficient signals for AI interpretation and commerce evaluation.
Does the AI Commerce Audit measure actual AI recommendations?
No. The audit measures the store side signals. Actual recommendation behavior is measured separately through Recommendation Intelligence.
How is an AI Commerce Audit different from GEO?
GEO optimizes content for generative visibility. An AI Commerce Audit measures the underlying commerce signals AI systems can use to understand and evaluate a merchant. A store can perform well in one and poorly in the other.
