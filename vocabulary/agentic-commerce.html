Definition

What is Agentic Commerce?

Agentic Commerce
Agentic Commerce is the emerging model where AI agents autonomously browse, compare, and complete purchases on behalf of buyers, without requiring human input at each step. The agent receives a buyer's intent, evaluates options, selects the best match, and completes the transaction.

The shift is already happening. Shopify launched its Agentic Dashboard in May 2026, generating llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and agents.md for every store. These files let AI agents read your product catalog and complete checkouts autonomously. Amazon retired Rufus and launched Alexa for Shopping, now embedded directly in Amazon search. ChatGPT Shopping, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity all have live checkout integrations.

The question is not whether agentic commerce is coming. It is already here. The question is whether your store is configured for it.

How Shopify Agentic Dashboard works

What Shopify generates for every store

📄
llms.txt
A plain text file that tells AI agents what your store sells, your brand positioning, and key product information. Shopify auto-generates this but content quality determines whether AI recommends you.
📋
llms-full.txt
Extended version of llms.txt with full product catalog data. AI agents use this to browse your products and match them to buyer queries without needing to crawl every product page.
🤖
agents.md
Instructions for AI agents on how to complete checkouts in your store. Defines the agentic checkout flow, payment methods, and transaction steps for autonomous purchasing.
⚠️
File quality matters
Shopify generates these files automatically but the default content is minimal. Stores that customize and enrich their llms.txt content score significantly higher than those with auto-generated defaults.

FAQ

Agentic Commerce questions

What is the Shopify Agentic Dashboard?▾
The Shopify Agentic Dashboard is a feature launched in May 2026 that automatically generates llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and agents.md for every Shopify store. These files enable AI agents to read your product catalog and complete checkout flows autonomously. Access it via Shopify Admin, Settings, Agentic.
Do I need to do anything to enable agentic commerce?▾
The Shopify Agentic Dashboard generates the required files automatically. However, the default content is minimal. To be competitive in agentic commerce, you need to enrich your llms.txt with specific product descriptions, brand positioning, and price ranges. You also need strong schema markup, trust signals, and commerce accuracy signals that AI agents use to evaluate your store.
What happens to stores that are not configured for agentic commerce?▾
Stores not configured for agentic commerce will be systematically bypassed as the model scales through 2026 and 2027. AI agents will recommend stores they can confidently read and transact with. Stores with poor llms.txt quality, missing schema markup, and unreadable trust signals will not be selected, regardless of product quality or brand recognition.
How is agentic commerce different from regular ecommerce?▾
In regular ecommerce, humans browse, compare, and decide. In agentic commerce, AI agents browse, compare, and decide, often completing the purchase without human input at each step. The buyer sets the intent ("find me a sustainable yoga mat under $60") and the AI agent handles the rest, including selecting the store, comparing options, and completing checkout.
