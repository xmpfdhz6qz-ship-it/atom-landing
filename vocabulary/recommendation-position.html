FAQ

Questions about Recommendation Position

Is position the same as Recommendation Share?
No. Share is how often you appear. Position is how strongly you are endorsed when you do. You can have high share and a weak position, which converts poorly.
Can I control my position directly?
Not directly, but you raise it by strengthening the trust and confidence signals AI uses to decide who to put first.
