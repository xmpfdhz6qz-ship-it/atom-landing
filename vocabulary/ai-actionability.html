What Is It

Reading is passive. Action requires certainty.

AI Actionability is the degree to which a store's data is structured precisely enough for an AI agent to take action on it: compare a price, confirm stock, apply a filter, or trigger a checkout. Readable content can still be unactionable if prices, variants, or availability are ambiguous or unstructured. Actionability is the bridge between extraction and transaction.

Why AI Cares

An agent that reads a vague price range cannot reliably compare or buy. Actionable data is exact, structured, and current: a numeric price in schema, a boolean availability, a defined variant. Without it, an agent either guesses or skips, and skipping is the safer choice for the agent.

Example

A product page shows a price described only as starting around $29 in text, but the actual variant prices live in a JavaScript widget. A human understands it. An agent cannot act on it. Putting exact prices and availability in Product schema makes the page AI Actionable.

FAQ

Questions about AI Actionability

What is AI Actionability?
AI Actionability is the degree to which a store's data is structured precisely enough for an AI agent to take action on it: compare a price, confirm stock, apply a filter, or trigger a checkout.
How is this different from AI Readability?
AI Readability is whether AI can extract and parse content at all. AI Actionability is whether what it extracts is precise enough to act on. Readable content can still be unactionable if prices, variants, or availability are ambiguous.
Why can't an AI agent act on 'around $30'?
An agent cannot reliably compare or buy against a range. It cannot verify whether the real price is 29, 31, or 34, and the safer choice for an agent facing that ambiguity is to skip the store entirely.
What makes data actionable?
Exact, structured, current values: a numeric price in schema, a boolean availability, a defined variant, a specific delivery window. Adjectives like affordable, fast, or popular are not actionable.
Where does Actionability sit in the framework?
It is the bridge between understanding and transacting. AI Readability lets AI see data, AI Understanding lets it interpret meaning, AI Actionability lets it decide, and Agent Transaction Readiness lets it complete the purchase.
