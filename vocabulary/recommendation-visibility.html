Definition
Recommendation Visibility is the binary and graded measure of whether a brand appears when AI systems process a relevant buyer query. Unlike Prompt Visibility, which measures appearance rate, Recommendation Visibility focuses on the specific query-response moment. A brand with high Recommendation Visibility is consistently surfaced in the queries that matter most to its niche.

A brand can exist in AI training data and still have zero Recommendation Visibility at query time. The gap between existing and being surfaced is where most brands lose revenue. A skincare brand known to AI but only appearing in broad queries has near zero Recommendation Visibility for a specific search like fragrance-free moisturizer for eczema under $50. Fixing semantic positioning raises it.

FAQ

Questions about Recommendation Visibility

What is Recommendation Visibility?
Recommendation Visibility is the binary and graded measure of whether a brand appears when AI systems process a relevant buyer query. It focuses on the specific query-response moment, not overall appearance rate.
How is Recommendation Visibility different from Prompt Visibility?
Prompt Visibility measures how often a brand shows up across a set of prompts. Recommendation Visibility measures the exact moment of a single query-response decision, whether AI included the brand this time, for this buyer.
Why can a well-known brand still have low Recommendation Visibility?
Existing in AI training data is not the same as being surfaced. A brand can be broadly known and still fall outside the narrow recommendation window a specific high-intent query opens.
What improves Recommendation Visibility fastest?
Semantic clarity and AI trust signals tend to move fastest. Sharpening how a brand's positioning maps to specific buyer language, and confirming trust signals AI can verify, both widen the recommendation window.
