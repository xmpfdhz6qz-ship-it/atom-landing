What Is It

Direction beats position.

Recommendation Velocity measures how quickly a brand's AI Commerce Score and Recommendation Share are changing over time. Positive velocity means the brand is becoming more visible. Negative velocity means competitors are gaining ground.

Why AI Cares

A static score tells you where you are. Velocity tells you which direction you are heading. A brand at 55 with positive velocity of plus 8 per month will overtake a brand at 68 with negative velocity, often sooner than anyone expects.

Example

In January a fashion brand scores 48. In March it scores 61. Recommendation Velocity is plus 6.5 per month. It will reach Moderate Confidence within two months at this pace.

FAQ

Questions about Recommendation Velocity

What is Recommendation Velocity?
Recommendation Velocity measures how quickly a brand's AI Commerce Score and Recommendation Share are changing over time. Positive velocity means the brand is becoming more visible to AI. Negative velocity means competitors are gaining ground.
Why does velocity matter more than the score itself?
A score is a snapshot. Velocity is the direction. A brand at 55 with velocity of plus 8 per month will overtake a brand at 68 with velocity of minus 3 per month, often within weeks. Position tells you where you are, velocity tells you where you are heading.
How is Recommendation Velocity calculated?
It tracks the rate of change across AI Commerce Score, Recommendation Share, Recommendation Confidence, and Coverage over a rolling monthly window, then expresses the combined trend as a single directional read: strong positive, positive, flat, or negative.
Can velocity turn negative even with a high score?
Yes. A brand can sit at a high score today while competitors close the gap faster than it grows. Velocity catches that decline early, before the score itself reflects the loss.
What actions move velocity the fastest?
Structural fixes that unlock new AI visibility tend to move fastest: adding FAQ schema, improving structured data, and expanding verified reviews. Each shows up as a measurable step change in Recommendation Velocity within weeks.
