FAQ

ChatGPT Visibility questions

What is ChatGPT Visibility?
ChatGPT Visibility is whether ChatGPT can identify your brand, understand what it sells, associate it with the right buyer intents, and confidently include it in a recommendation. It is broader than simply appearing in a response.
Is ChatGPT Visibility the same as AI Visibility?
ChatGPT Visibility is a platform-specific instance of the broader concept of AI Visibility. The underlying idea, being findable, identifiable, and understandable to an AI system, applies across ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, and other systems, but each platform evaluates it differently.
Can a store be visible but not recommended?
Yes, and this is the single most common gap merchants miss. A store can be found, identified, and even understood by ChatGPT, and still fail the trust or intent-matching checks that come after, which means it never gets recommended.
Why does ChatGPT recommend my competitor instead of me?
Usually because your competitor has stronger external evidence, clearer buyer-intent positioning, or fewer contradictions in their commercial facts, not because they rank higher on some hidden scoreboard. Recommendation is about confidence, and confidence comes from evidence.
Does structured data improve ChatGPT visibility?
It helps. Structured data like Product and Organization schema makes your commercial facts machine-verifiable, which supports the recommendation-confidence step. It is necessary but not sufficient on its own.
Does brand authority affect AI recommendations?
Yes. Independent evidence, reviews, editorial mentions, and community discussion, gives AI systems something to verify a brand against. Brands with no external presence are treated as unverified.
Does Reddit affect ChatGPT recommendations?
Reddit and other community discussion can contribute to the external-evidence layer of ChatGPT visibility, but it is one input among several, not a guaranteed lever. Treating it as the whole strategy overstates what any single signal can do.
Does llms.txt improve ChatGPT visibility?
llms.txt can help an AI system understand a store faster during retrieval, but it does not substitute for verifiable product data, trust signals, or intent-specific positioning. It is a small structural aid, not a visibility strategy.
How do I measure ChatGPT Recommendation Share?
By running a controlled set of real buyer-intent prompts against ChatGPT, extracting which brands get mentioned, and calculating how often your store appears relative to competitors across that intent set.
How often should I test my brand?
AI systems update on a rolling basis, and what they know about your brand changes as new evidence accumulates. Testing on a recurring basis, not once, is the only way to know whether your visibility is actually improving.
