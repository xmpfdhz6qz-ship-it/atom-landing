The evaluation pipeline

How ChatGPT evaluates every store in milliseconds

When a buyer submits a shopping query, ChatGPT runs every candidate brand through a four-step evaluation. Stores that pass all four steps get recommended. Stores that fail any step get excluded or mentioned weakly.

1
Entity Recognition
ChatGPT identifies which brands are relevant to the buyer query based on semantic intent, not keywords. "Best eco-friendly skincare" triggers different brand entities than "affordable skincare" even if both mention the same words.
Signals: brand name, category, semantic positioning
2
Trust Verification
ChatGPT cross-references training data for external validation. Reddit mentions in your niche, press coverage, Trustpilot or Google reviews, LinkedIn citations. Brands with no external presence are treated as unverified and excluded from confident recommendations.
Signals: Reddit, press, review platforms, external mentions
3
Intent Matching
ChatGPT scores how well each brand matches the specific buyer intent. A brand positioned as "premium minimalist skincare for sensitive skin" matches very differently from "great skincare products." Specificity in positioning determines whether your brand matches buyer queries.
Signals: positioning language, use-case specificity, llms.txt
4
Confidence Scoring
ChatGPT only recommends brands it can endorse confidently. Brands with unclear pricing, weak trust signals, or inconsistent information get excluded or mentioned with weak language. High confidence requires structured data, clear commerce signals, and strong external authority.
Signals: schema markup, price visibility, structured data

FAQ

ChatGPT visibility: common questions

How does ChatGPT decide which stores to recommend?▾
ChatGPT evaluates stores through four steps: entity recognition to identify relevant brands, trust verification using Reddit and press data, intent matching to score how well each brand fits the buyer query, and confidence scoring. Brands that pass all four steps get recommended confidently. Brands that fail any step get excluded or mentioned weakly.
How do I get my store recommended by ChatGPT?▾
The highest-impact actions are: build brand entity through Reddit presence and press coverage, add Organization schema and Product schema, make your brand positioning specific and buyer-intent-aligned, get reviews on Trustpilot or Google, and add llms.txt via Shopify Agentic Dashboard. These change what ChatGPT knows about your brand in its training data.
What is ChatGPT Shopping?▾
ChatGPT Shopping is OpenAI's product recommendation layer within ChatGPT. It launched paid product feed advertisements in early 2026. Organic recommendations still follow the brand entity evaluation model. Stores with strong brand entity, external validation, and structured data get recommended organically regardless of paid placement.
Can I pay to get recommended by ChatGPT?▾
Yes, ChatGPT Shopping launched paid product feed ads in early 2026. But paid placement supplements organic visibility, it does not replace it. Organic recommendations are based on brand entity and trust signals. A brand with weak organic signals and strong paid placement will still underperform a brand with strong organic visibility.
How long does it take to improve ChatGPT visibility?▾
ChatGPT visibility is tied to training data, which updates periodically, not in real time. Structural improvements like schema markup reflect faster because ChatGPT can crawl them. External authority building through Reddit and press takes longer as it requires accumulating mentions that feed into future training data. Expect 4 to 12 weeks for meaningful movement in ChatGPT Recommendation Share.
