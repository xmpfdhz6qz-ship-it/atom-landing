Commerce Knowledge Graph.

AI does not see a website. It sees a graph. Watch one node become a web of meaning.

Category Platform TermMaps Semantic understandingRelated AI Commerce Graph
Definition
The Commerce Knowledge Graph is the semantic knowledge structure that AI systems construct to understand the e-commerce internet. It connects merchant entities to product categories, use cases, buyer intents, price points, trust signals, and competitive relationships. Unlike the AI Commerce Graph, which maps recommendation flows, the Commerce Knowledge Graph maps semantic understanding. Atom Foundry measures each merchant's position and strength within this graph as part of the AI Commerce Score.
