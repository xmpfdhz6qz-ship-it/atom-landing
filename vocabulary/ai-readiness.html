What is the AI Readiness Score

A score that tells you exactly where you stand

AI Readiness Score
A 0 to 100 composite score measuring how confidently AI shopping agents can discover, understand, trust, and recommend your Shopify store to buyers. Higher score means higher probability of AI recommendation.

The AI Readiness Score directly predicts whether AI shopping agents like Alexa for Shopping, ChatGPT, and Google AI Mode will include your store in their recommendations when buyers ask relevant questions. Stores below 50 are systematically excluded. Stores above 85 are Highly Recommendable.

The 8 scoring factors · v3.0

What the score actually measures

AI Commerce Score v3.0: 8 factors, 100 points. Each factor is equally important in the 15% tier or weighted at 10% or 5%.

15%
Semantic Visuals & Image Clarity
AI vision systems read product images via GPT-4o Vision and compare against alt text. Generic alt tags like "product-image-4.jpg" cost you points. Added in v3.0.
15%
AI Structured Signals
Schema markup quality: Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, AggregateRating, price and currency schema, llms.txt quality. Single biggest factor. Average score very low across all stores.
15%
Core Technical & Interpretability
Crawler accessibility (GPTBot, ClaudeBot not blocked), Core Web Vitals via PageSpeed API, DOM structure quality and heading hierarchy. A technically broken store is invisible.
15%
AI Trust & Transaction Confidence
Machine-readable trust signals: return policy in crawlable HTML (not JS modal), checkout accessibility for agents, legal identity transparency. 64% of stores fail this.
15%
Commerce & Feed Accuracy
Price visible in HTML without JavaScript, schema:price and priceCurrency present, inventory availability consistent across feed and live page. 58% of stores fail this.
10%
User Intent Match
Semantic embedding comparison measuring how well product descriptions match actual buyer prompt language in that niche. Specific positioning wins. Generic copy matches nothing.
10%
Recommendation Confidence
AggregateRating schema with real ratingValue and reviewCount. Sentiment analysis on accessible review content. Determines how confidently AI endorses the store.
5%
External Authority Signals
Brand mentions in external LLM indexes, Reddit, Perplexity citations. Small weight now but expected to grow significantly by 2027 as LLM training data expands.
Where to start: AI Trust and Transaction Confidence and Commerce and Feed Accuracy fail in more than 60% of stores. Moving prices to server-rendered HTML and placing return policy in crawlable text are the two highest-impact fixes for most stores.

FAQ

AI Readiness Score questions

How is the AI Readiness Score calculated?▾
The AI Readiness Score v3.0 is calculated across 8 factors: Semantic Visuals and Image Clarity (15%), AI Structured Signals (15%), Core Technical and Interpretability (15%), AI Trust and Transaction Confidence (15%), Commerce and Feed Accuracy (15%), User Intent Match (10%), Recommendation Confidence (10%), and External Authority Signals (5%). Maximum score is 100. See the full methodology for details.
What is a good AI Readiness Score?▾
The average across 6,789 scanned stores is 45 out of 100. A score above 70 is Moderately Recommendable. Above 85 is Highly Recommendable. Currently, not one store in our dataset has reached 85 or above. Most stores have significant room to improve.
How do I improve my AI Readiness Score?▾
The fastest improvements: add Product JSON-LD schema markup, move prices to server-rendered HTML, add return policy in crawlable text, sharpen niche positioning, and improve product image alt text. Run a free scan at atomfoundry.dev to see your exact gaps.
How often is the AI Readiness Score updated?▾
Atom Foundry scans stores continuously. Your score reflects your store at the time of the most recent scan. As you make improvements, you can re-scan your store to see your updated score.
