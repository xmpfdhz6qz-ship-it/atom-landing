Definition
AI Recommendation Eligibility is the binary state of whether a store has met the minimum requirements to enter AI recommendation flows. It is not a score or a ranking. It is a gate. A store that fails the AI Trust Layer, has no structured data, or is blocked to AI crawlers is ineligible for recommendation regardless of product quality, brand recognition, or marketing spend.

Why AI Cares

Recommendation is not a spectrum for every store. For stores below the minimum threshold, it is binary: you are either in the recommendation pool or you are not. Eligibility must be established before any optimization is meaningful.

Before AI ranks anything, it has a pool of candidates: every store that could plausibly match the query. Most of that pool never makes it to ranking. Only stores that clear every gate stay in the set that gets ordered.

FAQ

Questions about AI Recommendation Eligibility

What is AI Recommendation Eligibility?
It is the threshold a store must clear before an AI agent will even consider recommending it. Recommendation is a two-stage process: first the agent filters stores down to those eligible, then it ranks the eligible ones. If you are below the eligibility line you are not ranked low, you are excluded entirely and never appear.
How is eligibility different from ranking?
Eligibility is a gate, ranking is an order. Eligibility decides whether you make it into the consideration set at all. Ranking then decides where you sit among the stores that qualified. Optimizing your ranking is pointless if you have not first cleared eligibility, because you are not yet in the set being ranked.
What does a store need to become eligible?
At minimum an AI agent needs to read your content, verify you are trustworthy, confirm what you sell, and match you to the buyer intent. These map to the readability, trust, structured-signal, and relevance factors in the AI Commerce Score. A store that is readable but unverifiable, or trustworthy but irrelevant to the query, stays below the threshold.
How do I know which gate is failing?
A free AI Commerce Score breaks your store into factors and shows the lowest one. Because eligibility is set by your weakest gate, that lowest factor is almost always what is keeping you out of recommendations, and it is where to start.
