What it is

A gate, not a ranking

AI Recommendation Eligibility
AI Recommendation Eligibility is the threshold a store must clear before an AI agent will even consider recommending it. Below the line you are not competing for a position, you are excluded from the consideration set.

Recommendation happens in two stages. First the agent filters every candidate store down to those that qualify. Then it ranks the ones that made it through. The mistake almost everyone makes is optimizing for the second stage while failing the first. If you have not cleared eligibility, there is no ranking to improve, because you are not yet in the set being ranked.

The line

Above or below the threshold

Eligibility is a line, not a slope. A store sits above it and competes, or below it and disappears. Two stores can be a hair apart on quality and land on opposite sides of the line, with completely different outcomes.

The weakest gate decides

Eligibility is your lowest gate

Because every gate has to pass, your eligibility is set by your weakest one, not your average. A store can be brilliantly readable, beautifully structured, and perfectly relevant, and still be ineligible because trust is unverifiable. One closed gate keeps you out, no matter how strong the others are.

Find the closed gate first. The fastest path to eligibility is not improving what already works, it is finding the single gate that is shut and opening it. That one change often moves a store from invisible in AI answers to suddenly present.

FAQ

AI Recommendation Eligibility: common questions

What is AI Recommendation Eligibility?▾
It is the threshold a store must clear before an AI agent will even consider recommending it. Recommendation is a two-stage process: first the agent filters stores down to those eligible, then it ranks the eligible ones. If you are below the eligibility line you are not ranked low, you are excluded entirely and never appear.
How is eligibility different from ranking?▾
Eligibility is a gate, ranking is an order. Eligibility decides whether you make it into the consideration set at all. Ranking then decides where you sit among the stores that qualified. Optimizing your ranking is pointless if you have not first cleared eligibility, because you are not yet in the set being ranked.
What does a store need to become eligible?▾
At minimum an AI agent needs to read your content, verify you are trustworthy, confirm what you sell, and match you to the buyer intent. These map to the readability, trust, structured-signal, and relevance factors in the AI Commerce Score. A store that is readable but unverifiable, or trustworthy but irrelevant to the query, stays below the threshold.
How do I know which gate is failing?▾
A free AI Commerce Score breaks your store into factors and shows the lowest one. Because eligibility is set by your weakest gate, that lowest factor is almost always what is keeping you out of recommendations, and it is where to start.
