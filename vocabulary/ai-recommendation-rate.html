What Is It

How often do you exist? Not how much you own.

AI Recommendation Rate is the percentage of tested purchase-intent prompts in which a merchant appears as a recommendation, in any position and at any confidence level. It is the broadest measure of AI visibility. A merchant with a high AI Recommendation Rate appears across many queries. A merchant with a low rate is systematically excluded from most recommendation flows in its category.

Why AI Cares

AI Recommendation Rate is the most direct measurement available of whether AI commerce optimization is working. It converts every technical improvement into a measurable business outcome: did our brand appear in more buyer conversations this month.

Example

A home goods brand tests 100 purchase-intent prompts relevant to its niche. It appears in 23. Its AI Recommendation Rate is 23 percent. After fixing schema markup and niche positioning it retests and scores 41 percent. The rate improvement is directly attributable to the optimizations made.

FAQ

Questions about AI Recommendation Rate

What is AI Recommendation Rate?
AI Recommendation Rate is the percentage of tested purchase-intent prompts in which a merchant appears as a recommendation, in any position and at any confidence level. It is the broadest measure of AI visibility: does the AI ever say your name.
How is AI Recommendation Rate different from Recommendation Share?
Recommendation Share measures how much of the market you own, weighted by category relevance. AI Recommendation Rate asks a simpler yes-or-no question across a fixed prompt set: did AI mention you at all. Rate is the entry-level metric; Share is the competitive one.
Is a high AI Recommendation Rate the same as a high ranking?
No. Recommendation Rate only tracks whether you were mentioned, not where. A brand mentioned last in every response and a brand mentioned first in every response can have the same rate. Position and confidence are separate metrics layered on top.
Does AI Recommendation Rate vary by platform?
Yes, significantly. The same store can score 41 percent on ChatGPT and 18 percent on Claude, because each system indexes, retrieves, and reasons about merchants differently. Rate should always be reported per platform, never as a single blended number.
What increases AI Recommendation Rate?
The inputs AI checks before deciding whether to mention a brand at all: structured schema data, verifiable trust signals, review volume and quality, clear policies, and tight match between buyer intent and product language. Each one moves the rate independently.
