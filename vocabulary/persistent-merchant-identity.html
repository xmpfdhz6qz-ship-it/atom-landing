Persistent Merchant Identity

Every AI system forms its own picture of your brand. When those pictures match, AI recommends you with confidence. When they clash, recommendations fall apart.

Category Platform TermShort PMIRelated AI Trust Layer
Definition
Persistent Merchant Identity measures how consistently and accurately AI systems understand and represent a brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Alexa for Shopping, and Apple Intelligence. A brand with low PMI gets understood differently by different systems, resulting in inconsistent recommendation outcomes.

What breaks it

Three ways identity fragments

Your site and your schema disagree
Your homepage says one thing, your Organization schema says another, your llms.txt says a third. AI reads all of them and cannot decide which is true, so it trusts none of them fully.
External mentions tell a different story
Your site positions you as premium endurance nutrition, but Reddit talks about you as a budget option and press covers you as a weight loss product. AI weighs the outside world heavily, so the mismatch drags your identity apart.
Your positioning keeps changing
A brand that rewrites its story every season never lets any AI system lock in a stable picture. Consistency over time is what builds a persistent identity, not a single perfect description.
How to build it

Four signals that make identity stick

01
Organization schema
A machine readable statement of who you are, repeated on every page. The anchor every AI system reads first.
02
A clear llms.txt
Define your niche, your products, and who you serve in plain language built for AI agents.
03
A real about page
Crawlable text that states your story once, clearly, so every system extracts the same facts.
04
Consistent external presence
Reviews, press, and mentions that echo the same positioning your own site uses.
Example
An outdoor brand states premium hiking gear for cold climates in its schema, its llms.txt, its about page, and it shows up the same way in press and reviews. Every AI system builds the same picture. High PMI. It gets recommended consistently across every platform.
FAQ

Questions about Persistent Merchant Identity

Is PMI a score I can measure?
Atom Foundry reads the signals that build it, such as Organization schema, about page presence, and consistency of your structured data, and reports identity strength as part of your scan.
Why do different AI tools describe me differently?
Each one builds its understanding independently from the signals it can read. If those signals are inconsistent across the web, each system lands on a different picture. Fixing the inconsistency is how you bring them back in line.
How does PMI relate to trust?
They work together. A brand AI cannot pin down consistently is a brand AI hesitates to recommend. See the AI Trust Layer for the verification step that sits alongside identity.
