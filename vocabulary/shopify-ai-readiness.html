What Shopify added, and what it doesn't do

Shopify gave every store the doorway

Starting in early May 2026, Shopify began serving llms.txt, llms-full.txt, and agents.md by default on every store; a later May update made agents.md the canonical AI discovery file, with llms.txt and llms-full.txt redirecting to it. That's a real, platform-wide step. But a file existing is not the same as a store being understood.

FAQ

Shopify AI readiness: common questions

Does this overlap with Shopify SEO?
Some AI readiness improvements overlap with SEO. Adding schema markup can improve rich snippets in Google results, and specific niche positioning language helps both AI intent matching and SEO keyword relevance. But most AI readiness changes, like llms.txt customization, price visibility in HTML, and return policy text, have no significant SEO impact. They're purely for AI shopping agent optimization.
Do I need a developer to fix these issues?
Most of the highest-impact fixes (Product JSON-LD, AggregateRating schema, return policy text) can be added through a schema app or a small theme.liquid edit without custom development. llms.txt and agents.md customization is now available directly through the Shopify theme editor.
Does Shopify's Agentic Foundation replace the need for this?
No. The Agentic Foundation gives every store the discovery files AI agents look for first. It doesn't add Product schema, fix JavaScript-rendered prices, or write specific buyer-intent copy. Those are still store-level fixes.
How is this different from my Shopify SEO app's score?
SEO apps score against search engine ranking factors. This audit scores against what an AI shopping agent needs to identify, verify, trust, and act on a product, which is a different, narrower, and stricter set of checks.
How often should I re-run this audit?
After any theme change, app change affecting reviews or pricing, or Shopify platform update to the Agentic Foundation. Outside of that, a quarterly check is enough for most stores.
