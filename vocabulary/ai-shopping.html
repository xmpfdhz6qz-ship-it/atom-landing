How AI shopping works

The new buying journey runs through AI

The old buying journey: human searches Google, scrolls results, clicks, compares, buys. The new buying journey: human asks AI, AI evaluates stores, AI recommends one, buyer buys — or AI buys autonomously on their behalf.

When a buyer asks "what's the best sustainable yoga mat under $80?" AI does not search Google. It evaluates stores it has already indexed based on structured product data, trust signals, semantic clarity, and commerce accuracy. The store it can most confidently understand and verify gets recommended. The rest are invisible.

🗣️
Buyer asks AI
Instead of typing keywords into Google, buyers ask AI assistants for product recommendations in natural language. Specific, intent-rich queries.
🔍
AI evaluates stores
AI agents scan structured data, trust signals, reviews, pricing, and semantic content across thousands of stores to find the best match for the buyer's query.
⭐
AI recommends one
AI presents one or a handful of stores it can confidently recommend. The store with the strongest AI signals wins. Stores with weak or missing signals are not shown.
💳
AI completes purchase
In agentic flows, AI completes the purchase autonomously via llms.txt and agents.md. In recommendation flows, the buyer clicks through and converts at 50% higher rates.
The major AI shopping platforms

Who is driving AI shopping in 2026

1
Alexa for Shopping — 300M+ users
Amazon retired Rufus in May 2026 and launched Alexa for Shopping, embedded directly in Amazon search. Now the largest AI shopping platform. Evaluates external Shopify stores via Product schema and trust signals.
2
ChatGPT Shopping
Product feed ads live in 2026. ChatGPT recommends stores in response to buyer queries. Strong semantic positioning and Organization schema are key ranking signals.
3
Google AI Mode and AI Overviews
Google's AI layer now surfaces product recommendations before organic results. Rich snippets and AggregateRating schema are critical for inclusion. Stores without schema are excluded.
4
Perplexity Shopping
Real-time product search with AI-generated recommendations. Crawls and evaluates stores in real time. External mentions and trust signals heavily weighted.
FAQ

AI Shopping questions

How do I get my Shopify store recommended by AI shopping agents?▾
The fastest path to AI recommendation: add Product JSON-LD schema markup, enable AggregateRating schema for reviews, make prices visible in HTML, add machine-readable return and shipping policies, and improve your niche positioning. Run a free AI Readiness Score scan at atomfoundry.dev to see your exact gaps.
Does Shopify automatically optimize my store for AI shopping?▾
Shopify's Agentic Dashboard generates llms.txt and agents.md files for every store — a foundation for AI shopping. But most of the signals AI shopping agents evaluate (Product schema, AggregateRating, pricing in HTML, trust signals) require manual setup or apps. Most Shopify themes do not include these by default.
How much traffic does AI shopping drive?▾
Shopify confirmed in Q1 2026 that AI-referred orders grew 13 times year over year. AI-referred orders convert at 50% higher rates and carry 14% higher average order value compared to other traffic sources. The channel is growing rapidly and becoming a significant revenue driver for optimized stores.
Will AI shopping replace Google?▾
AI shopping is not replacing Google — it is adding a new layer on top of it. Google itself is integrating AI into search results via AI Mode and AI Overviews. Stores that optimize for both traditional SEO and AI recommendation signals will capture traffic from both channels. Stores that ignore AI optimization will lose an increasing share of discovery.
