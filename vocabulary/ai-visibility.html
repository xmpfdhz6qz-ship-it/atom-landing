Definition
AI Visibility is the foundational measure of whether an e-commerce store exists in the eyes of AI shopping systems. A store with high AI Visibility is discoverable, interpretable, and surfaceable by AI agents when relevant buyer queries are submitted. AI Visibility is necessary but not sufficient for recommendation. A store can be visible to AI and still not get recommended.

The shift from search-first to recommendation-first commerce means that visibility in AI systems is now the most important form of digital discoverability. Stores that are invisible to AI are invisible to a growing share of buyers.

FAQ

Questions about AI Visibility

What is AI Visibility for e-commerce stores?
AI Visibility is whether an AI shopping agent can read your store at all. A store has AI Visibility when its content is crawlable, structured, and parseable by AI systems like ChatGPT, Alexa for Shopping, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity. Without AI Visibility, a store cannot be recommended to buyers regardless of how good its products are.
What is the difference between AI Visibility and AI Recommendability?
AI Visibility means an AI agent can read your store. AI Recommendability means an AI agent will confidently recommend your store to a buyer. A store can be fully visible to AI and still not get recommended, because recommendation requires intent matching, trust verification, and commerce accuracy on top of basic visibility. Visibility is the floor. Recommendability is what drives revenue.
How do I know if my store is invisible to AI?
Run a free AI Commerce Score scan. A low score signals your store is in AI Invisible Risk territory. You can also do a quick manual check by opening your product page source and looking for JSON-LD schema markup and visible prices. If neither is present, AI agents are reading very little from your store.
Why are most stores invisible to AI?
Most stores were built for human visitors and search engine results, not for AI recommendation engines. They lack structured data, machine readable trust signals, and semantic clarity, so AI agents skip them even when the underlying products are strong.
Is AI Visibility the same as GEO or ACO?
AI Visibility is a component of both. GEO, Generative Engine Optimization, covers getting cited by AI systems broadly. ACO, Agentic Commerce Optimization, covers getting recommended in AI shopping flows specifically. AI Visibility is the foundation both require.
How fast can AI Visibility be improved?
Schema markup can be added in an afternoon and AI agents will begin reading it within days. Pricing visibility fixes are usually a few hours of development work. Unlike traditional SEO, AI Visibility improvements show measurable results quickly because AI agents re-crawl frequently.
