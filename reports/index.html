What's in a report

Every report follows the same format

01
Prompts
20 buyer scenarios, 400 observations
The kind a real shopper types before buying, not generic brand questions. Each of the 20 best-of, comparison, budget, and use-case prompts is run 20 times to clear the noise floor.
02
Model
ChatGPT, single model
These free snapshots measure one model at full observation depth. Our paid Recommendation Intelligence report checks ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini together, tracked on an ongoing basis.
03
Findings
2 to 3 key findings
Not every observation, just what actually stood out: how often the brand came up, and who it got named alongside.
04
Access
Free, public, unedited
No signup, no gate. Published as-is, findings and all, good or bad for the brand measured.
