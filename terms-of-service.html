Automated Data Collection & Web Crawling

Public data only. Atom Foundry exclusively collects and analyzes publicly accessible data. We do not attempt to access password-protected areas, private databases, authenticated content, or any data that is not freely available to the general public and standard web crawlers.

Respect for technical signals. Our crawler respects standard technical signals including robots.txt directives where technically feasible.
