AI agents are already visiting your store.

Now you can see them.

AI Agent Snapshot shows you which AI agents visit your store, what they read, which pages they reach, how often they return, and when they were last seen. Real detection. Not estimates.

Launching as a Shopify app. Built first for the stores where AI commerce is happening, coming to websites next.

LIVE ON OUR LAB SHOP, MINIMAL WEAR founder.atomfoundry.dev
The idea in one line

Think Google Analytics, but for AI agents.

GA4 shows you human traffic. AI Agent Snapshot shows you the traffic your existing analytics cannot see.

Who
Which AI agent visited: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and more, identified by name, not just flagged as a generic bot.
Where
Which pages it read on your store, down to the exact URL.
When
The exact time of the visit, and how long ago the agent was last seen.
How
Whether it browsed pages directly or pulled data through a feed.
Why
The likely intent behind the visit, such as checking a product, a price or a policy.
What it viewed
Which specific products, collections or policies it looked at.
What it did
Whether it just read the page, or the visit led to a citation or a recommendation.

No dashboard to babysit

Just a signal. And an inbox.

No dashboard to babysit. No reports to interpret. When an AI agent reads your store, you see it.

One small icon lives quietly in your admin, visible only to you and your team, no one else. Click it, and a few dials fan out at the edge of the screen, like an instrument cluster. Click again, they are gone. Click through and you can open the full view for a complete history. The rest happens on its own: the moment an AI agent reads your store, an email goes out that says exactly what happened.
